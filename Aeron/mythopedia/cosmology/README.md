- `mana.md` — mana as Creator's lifeblood; the substance flowing through every tier.
- `pantheons.md` — tier 4 structural canon (five canonical pantheons; pantheon-leader gods; descent and elevation).
- `mortal_tier.md` — tier 6 slot inventory (mundane races, heroes, demi-gods, angels, spirits, monsters, magic, pantheon-worship structures, mortal-bound gods).
