# Mortal Tier (Tier 6)

The mortal tier is the layer of life on Aeron. It is the only tier most mortals will ever know directly; it is also the tier that **inherits from every layer above** and therefore the only tier where the full mythic hierarchy compounds into ordinary experience. This page is the structural slot-list for tier 6. It does not populate the slots; it names them, marks where each inherits from above, and notes where each is universal vs continent-local.

Per-slot canon, named species, named mage-orders, named mortal sovereignties, and the like are downstream and continent-local. The framework here is universal across Aeron.

## The Tier-6 Doctrine

Tier 6 is the **only tier where descent rules dominate over direct creation**. Above tier 6, most named beings are direct Creator-creations (Aru/Loran, all Vatharen, all pantheon-leader gods, all Vorothar, all Seravel, Solaryth, Nyxorys). At tier 6, only the humanic and demonic Adam/Eve are direct Creator-creations; everything else descends, derives, or is elevated.

Tier 6 is also the tier where **continent-locality is structurally maximal**. The framework rules above (the same Vatharen, the same Vorothar, the same five pantheons, the same Seravel, the same Creator) are universal; the populated content beneath them (which gods are worshipped, which races thrive, which heroes rise, which mage-orders teach) is continental. A page that names tier-6 specifics is a continent-local page; a page that names the framework is a universal page.

## Slot Inventory

Each slot below names: what it is, where it inherits from above, whether the slot itself is universal or continent-local, and a one-paragraph intent. Slots are listed in approximate descent order — the closer to the top, the closer to direct Creator-creation among tier-6 beings.

### Mundane Races

- **What it is:** the standard living mortal beings of Aeron — humanic, demonic, and other mundane species.
- **Inherits from:** tier 6 itself (humanic/demonic Adam/Eve as direct Creator-creations); other mundane races descend from beasts (lesser kin of `Vorothar`), from Creator-created mortal-stock outside Eden, or from cross-lineages with descended gods.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **Universal at the framework level** (humanic/demonic axis applies across Aeron); **continent-local at the population level** (which subspecies of humanic, which subspecies of demonic, and which other mundane races thrive where).
- **Intent:** the slot is the daily mortal life of Aeron. It is the largest population by count, the shortest-lived per individual, and the slot through which all mortal civilization is built. Most stories of mortal heroism, daily ritual, governance, art, and ordinary love begin here.

### Heroes

- **What it is:** mortals whose lives have crossed cosmic-significance threshold and whose Class 2 fated-being star is now traceable in the sky.
- **Inherits from:** the [stars](../astrology/stars.md) doctrine (Class 2 fated-being stars), the [hero-summoning mechanic](../mechanics/hero_summoning.md), and the cosmic personalities of the gods or beasts the hero is in relation with.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local in identity** (this hero, in this culture, in this generation); **universal in mechanism** (every continent has heroes; the Class 2 star doctrine applies to all).
- **Intent:** heroes are the engine by which mortal life touches the cosmic without ascending to it. A hero is a fated being while alive. Some heroes are elevated to demi-godhood after their living years; most are not. The slot exists to give mortal stories cosmic stakes without requiring every mortal to be cosmic.

### Demi-Gods

- **What it is:** beings of mixed mortal-and-divine descent, or fully elevated heroes who have crossed the qualitative threshold of [tier-4 elevation](pantheons.md) and now occupy a cluster-pattern within a galaxy.
- **Inherits from:** [Pantheons (tier 4)](pantheons.md) for elevation; [stars](../astrology/stars.md) Class 2 transitioning to galaxy-cluster pattern; mortal-tier descent for the mortal half (where applicable).
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local in identity** (each demi-god belongs to a particular pantheon and a particular continent's worship); **universal in mechanism** (every pantheon has demi-godhood pathways).
- **Intent:** demi-gods bridge the mortal and the divine. They are the cosmic recognition that the line between "great hero" and "lesser god" is real but porous. The slot allows for the entry of new beings into pantheons over mortal time.

### Angels

- **What it is:** servants and emanations of pantheon-tier gods, often heralding, mediating, or acting on the personal will of a god across mortal experience.
- **Inherits from:** [Pantheons (tier 4)](pantheons.md); each angel descends from or emanates from a specific god.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local** (an angel is the angel of a particular god of a particular pantheon). The slot itself is universal; the contents are continental.
- **Intent:** angels make the gods present in mortal affairs without the gods themselves leaving their galaxy-clusters. The angelic slot exists for messaging, mediation, intervention, and witness on the gods' behalf. Angels typically do not have Class 2 stars; they are not fated mortals; they are extensions of divine will.

### Spirits

- **What it is:** the smaller, place-bound or person-bound non-mortal beings of Aeron — household spirits, ancestral presences, lesser place-genii, talisman-inhabiting essences, sworn-oath shades.
- **Inherits from:** scattered sources — minor lesser-kin of [`Athravenel` (Wild-Heart)](../legendary_beasts/athravenel.md) for place-spirits; mortal souls retained beyond breath for ancestral spirits; mage-craft for talisman essences; cross-lineages with descended pantheon-emanations for sworn-oath spirits.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local in population** (which spirits dwell where); **universal in slot existence** (every continent has spirits in the broad sense).
- **Intent:** spirits are the cosmic-low-frequency presence in mortal daily life. They are smaller than gods, smaller than angels, smaller than legendary beasts. They are the cosmic acknowledgment that **place, ancestry, and oath persist in cosmically-real forms even at small scale**.

### Monsters

- **What it is:** lesser legendary beasts and other non-mortal predator-form beings smaller than the [`Vorothar`](../legendary_beasts/README.md) but larger than mundane wildlife.
- **Inherits from:** [Tier 5 (`Vorothar` and lesser kin)](../legendary_beasts/README.md) for most monsters; [Bloodborn-Sire](../legendary_beasts/krevoraen.md) and [Hellspawn-Sire](../legendary_beasts/vorthalen.md) lineages for the cursed-humanoid and fire-fiend monster classes specifically; spirit-cross lineages for the rarer haunt-monsters.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local in distribution** (which monsters dwell where, in what numbers); **universal in framework** (the lineage rules from `Vorothar` apply to all).
- **Intent:** monsters are the cosmic threats that mortal civilization can sometimes oppose with mortal force. Unlike `Vorothar`, monsters can be killed by mortal might (in extremis); unlike mundane animals, monsters carry mana-flavor from a `Vorothar` lineage and are cosmically-significant in folk-memory.

### Magic (Mortal Use Of Mana)

- **What it is:** the mortal practice of working with [mana](mana.md). Includes mage-orders, ritual traditions, talisman-craft, divination, and the various tier-aligned mage-disciplines.
- **Inherits from:** [Mana doctrine](mana.md) directly; specific traditions inherit from the tier and entity their mana-flavor draws from (a moon-mage from the Seravel; a Vatharen-attuned mage from the relevant Vatharen; a beast-mage from the relevant Vorothar; a god-priest from the pantheon-deity).
- **Universal or continent-local:** **universal in substance** (mana is the same Creator's-lifeblood everywhere); **continent-local in tradition** (which mage-orders teach what; which rituals are practiced where; which disciplines have prestige).
- **Intent:** magic is mortal participation in the cosmic. The slot exists to allow individual mortals (or mage-orders of mortals) to work with the cosmic in trained, disciplined, sometimes dangerous ways. **Magic is not a separate substance; it is mortal use of mana.** The magic-doctrine page is the [mana](mana.md) doctrine.

### Pantheons-as-Worship-Structures (Continental)

- **What it is:** the institutionalized worship of [tier-4 pantheons](pantheons.md) within a particular continent's mortal civilization. This includes: temples, priestly orders, ritual calendars, prayer-language, sacred sites, and the political role of religion.
- **Inherits from:** [Pantheons (tier 4)](pantheons.md) directly. The pantheon is the universal entity; its continental worship-structure is its tier-6 mortal expression.
- **Universal or continent-local:** entirely **continent-local**. Each continent's worship-structure for each pantheon is its own.
- **Intent:** the slot exists because mortal civilization institutionalizes the worship of cosmic beings. The pantheon-as-galaxy is universal; the temple-of-the-pantheon-in-this-kingdom is local. Religious traditions, sect-boundaries, syncretic blends, and civic-religious politics all live here.

### Mortal-Bound Gods (Rare)

- **What it is:** beings of god-tier personality whose pattern is **not** in any galaxy and is instead anchored to mortal soil — gods of a single mountain, gods of a single sacred well, gods of a single dynasty.
- **Inherits from:** unusual cases. Most are: extraordinarily faded pantheon-gods whose galaxy-cluster has dimmed below visibility but whose mortal worship persists at small scale; or elevated heroes who never fully ascended into a galaxy and remain demi-bound to their mortal continent; or place-tied spirits that have grown to god-scale through long sustained worship.
- **Universal or continent-local:** **continent-local by definition** (the slot exists because the being is anchored to a mortal place rather than a galaxy).
- **Intent:** the slot exists because not every god-tier being lives in a galaxy. The slot is rare. It is the cosmic acknowledgment that mortal worship can sometimes sustain a god outside the canonical galaxy-pantheon structure — at a smaller scale, with a smaller reach, but real.

## Tier 6 Inheritance Map

The mortal tier draws from every tier above. The following table summarizes the most common inheritance-channels:

| Tier 6 slot | Primary inheritance | Secondary inheritance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mundane Races | Tier-6 Adam/Eve (direct Creator-creation) | Beast-descent (via lesser `Vorothar` kin), god-descent (via lesser pantheon kin) |
| Heroes | Stars (Class 2) | Pantheon (gods take interest in some heroes); Vorothar (rarely, beasts take interest) |
| Demi-Gods | Pantheons (tier 4 elevation) | Stars (Class 2 → galaxy-cluster transition); mundane race (mortal half) |
| Angels | Pantheons (tier 4 emanation) | none |
| Spirits | Wild-Heart (lesser place-genii); mortal souls (ancestral); mage-craft (talisman) | varies |
| Monsters | Vorothar lineages (lesser kin) | Spirit cross-lineage (rare haunt-monsters) |
| Magic | Mana (universal substance) | Each tradition's source-tier (moons, Vatharen, gods, beasts) |
| Pantheon-Worship-Structures | Pantheons (tier 4) | Mortal civilization (tier 6 itself) |
| Mortal-Bound Gods | Faded pantheon-gods, un-ascended heroes, grown-up spirits | varies |

## Universal-vs-Continent Seam at Tier 6

Tier 6 is the only tier where the seam is **explicitly continental at the population level by design**. Universal facts about tier 6 (the framework above; the humanic/demonic axis; the Class 2 star mechanism; mana as substance) are uncontroversial. Continental facts (which gods are worshipped here; which races dwell here; which heroes are remembered here; which mage-orders teach here; which spirits inhabit which villages) are the product of continental history and mythic tradition.

When in canon doubt, ask: is this fact about the *framework* or about the *populated content*? Framework facts are universal; populated facts are continental.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Tier 6 is the layer of mortal life on Aeron.
- Tier 6's only direct Creator-creations are the humanic and demonic Adam/Eve in the unreachable [Eden](../places/eden.md).
- Every other tier-6 being descends, derives, is elevated, or is constructed via mortal craft.
- The slot inventory enumerated here — Mundane Races, Heroes, Demi-Gods, Angels, Spirits, Monsters, Magic, Pantheon-Worship-Structures, Mortal-Bound Gods — is the canonical full set of tier-6 slot categories.
- Each slot's universal-vs-continent-local marker is locked as stated.
- Magic is mortal use of mana, not a separate substance.
- Mortal-bound gods exist as a rare slot but are not the default for god-tier beings; the default is galaxy-pantheon residence.

## Canon Boundaries

- This page slots tier 6; it does not populate it. Continent-local content is downstream.
- Specific named mage-orders, mundane races, monsters, demi-gods, angels, spirits, heroes, and pantheon-worship-structures are downstream and continent-local.
- The relationship between tier-6 inheritance and the [stars](../astrology/stars.md) doctrine (especially Class 2 hero stars and their possible elevation) is fully treated in the [hero-summoning mechanic](../mechanics/hero_summoning.md) page.
- Whether certain mundane races are descended from beasts (e.g., dragonborn from Velkoraen-kin) vs from humanic/demonic firsts vs from a separate Creator-creation outside Eden is open and continent-local.
- The tier-6 metaphysics of soul, afterlife, reincarnation, and the post-death pathways into spirits, ancestors, demi-gods, or further mortal lives is open and is partly addressed in the hero-summoning canon and partly continental tradition.
