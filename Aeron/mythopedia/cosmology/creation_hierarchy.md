# Direct vs Derived Creation Hierarchy

This page enumerates which classes of being in Aeron are **direct Creator-creations** and which are **derived** (descended, elevated, emanated, dream-seeded, hybrid-cross, or mortal-craft-produced). The distinction is the spine of narrative authority claims: direct creations have unique mana-flavors and ontological independence; derived beings inherit dilute mana-flavor and trace lineage upward.

## Doctrine

The cosmic stack of Aeron is **mostly direct Creator-creation at the top and increasingly derived at the bottom**. Above tier 6, almost every named being is direct. At tier 6, only the [Firsts](../entities/firsts.md) are direct; everything else descends. This page lists the canonical breakdown.

A being is **directly created** if the Creator made them. A being is **derived** if they came into existence by any of:

- **Descent** from a directly-created being (children, lineage continuation).
- **Elevation** from a lower-tier being (heroes elevated to demi-godhood; mortal-mages elevated to angelic mediator status).
- **Emanation** from a directly-created being (an aspect-shed; a sub-pattern that crystallizes from the parent).
- **Dream-seeding** by a `Vorothar` (the lesser-kin pathway specific to tier 5).
- **Hybrid-cross** between two directly-created lineages.
- **Mortal craft** (talisman essences, oath-spirits, summoned beings).

## Direct Creator-Creations (Canonical Inventory)

The following classes are **direct Creator-creations**:

### Tier 1
- [Aru](../entities/aru.md) (the Creator before [Orun](../events/first_pulse.md))
- [Loran](../entities/loran.md) (the Creator after Orun, in dynamic harmonic state — **the same being as Aru**, not a second tier-1 entity)

### Luminaries (Taviran)
- [Solaryth](../astrology/luminaries.md) — light-luminary
- [Nyxorys](../astrology/luminaries.md) — darklight-luminary

### Tier 2 (Seravel)
- All seven moons: `Serenel`, `Seramor`, `Tavrekal`, `Taviril`, `Kethorel`, `Oraemor`, `Serathil`

### Tier 3 (Vatharen)
- All eight Vatharen: `Vaelvor`, `Vaelmor`, `Taelden`, `Taelmoth`, `Yirven`, `Yirmor`, `Aenel`, `Aenor`

### Tier 4 (Pantheon-Leader Gods)
- The five canonical pantheon-leaders. Specific names await per-continent canon.

### Tier 5 (Vorothar — Greater Legendary Beasts)
- All fourteen Vorothar: `Velkoraen`, `Mirathven`, `Vothorisk`, `Drathalon`, `Khoramor`, `Phaelvenor`, `Skaelmorath`, `Drovenrath`, `Aelvorath`, `Vesselrath`, `Khaethorom`, `Vorthalen`, `Athravenel`, `Krevoraen`

### Tier 6 (Firsts)
- The four Firsts: humanic Adam, humanic Eve, demonic Adam, demonic Eve. Dwell in [Eden](../places/eden.md).

### Mortal Lineage-Anchor Extensions
- Creator-extension instances that produced the original mortal-lineage stocks outside Eden are canonically direct Creator-creations as well, but they are **not** named beings; they are the ancestors-of-ancestors of mortal civilization, cosmically present in folk-memory only as "the first humanic generation outside Eden" and "the first demonic generation outside Eden." See [Firsts](../entities/firsts.md) for the descent doctrine.

That is the canonical full set of direct Creator-creations on Aeron at present canon. Any future direct Creator-creation must be canonized explicitly; **no being is silently elevated to direct-creation status**.

## Derived Beings

### By Descent

**Mortal lineages** descend from the [Firsts](../entities/firsts.md) (and from Creator-extension stock outside Eden). Each humanic mortal carries dilute humanic-Adam-or-Eve mana-flavor; each demonic mortal carries dilute demonic-Adam-or-Eve mana-flavor.

**Lesser legendary beast kin** descend from `Vorothar` parents. Direct-generation, emanation, dream-seeding, and hybrid-cross are all canonical pathways. Each lesser kin inherits dilute mana-flavor from its Greater parent.

**Lesser gods** descend from pantheon-leader gods. Direct-generation (god-children of god-parents), emanation, and aspect-shed are canonical pathways within tier 4.

### By Elevation

**Demi-gods** can be heroes elevated from tier 6 by Class 2 fated-being star ascension into a galaxy. See [pantheons](pantheons.md) and [hero-summoning](../mechanics/hero_summoning.md).

**Angels** are emanations of pantheon-tier gods, descended/derived rather than directly Creator-created (with the rare exceptional case of pantheon-leader-emanations being canonical at certain pantheon thresholds — open per pantheon).

### By Hybrid-Cross

Cross-lineages between two directly-created lineages produce derived hybrids:
- `Vorothar`-cross hybrids (e.g., `Velkoraen` × `Aelvorath` = storm-wyrms; `Vorthalen` × `Drathalon` = balrogs proper).
- Inter-pantheon god-children (rare, continent-local).
- Mortal-divine cross-lineages (mortals with one god-parent — these are the foundation of many demi-god pathways).
- Mortal-beast cross-lineages (mortal-beast children that descend with mixed mana-flavors).

### By Mortal Craft

**Talisman-essences**, **oath-spirits**, and **summoned beings** can be created by sufficiently skilled mortal mage-orders. These are derived in a strong sense — they are *brought into existence by mortal action*, not by Creator action — but they are still rooted in the mana flowing through the cosmic stack.

## What Is Not a Direct Creator-Creation

By contrast, the following classes are **never** direct Creator-creations in current canon:

- **Lesser legendary beasts** — always descended from a `Vorothar`.
- **Dragons and other beast-descendant classes** — always descended from a `Vorothar`.
- **Mundane mortals** — always descended from the Firsts or from Creator-extension stock.
- **Heroes** — always mortal in origin.
- **Demi-gods** — always elevated or descended.
- **Angels** — always pantheon-emanated.
- **Spirits** — always derived from beasts, mortals, mage-craft, or place-spirits.
- **Mortal-bound gods** — always derived (faded pantheon-gods, un-ascended heroes, grown-up spirits).

If folk-memory or mortal tradition claims direct Creator-creation for a being not on the canonical inventory above, the claim is **mortal mythic exaggeration**, not canon.

## Why the Distinction Matters

The direct-vs-derived distinction matters for several canonical reasons:

1. **Mana-flavor uniqueness.** Direct creations have unique mana-flavors. Derived beings have dilute or composite flavors. A mortal mage who wishes to access a unique flavor must attune to a direct-creation being.

2. **Ontological independence.** Direct creations exist by Creator-fiat. Their continued existence does not depend on any other being beneath the Creator. Derived beings depend on their lineage-anchors (Firsts, Vorothar, gods).

3. **Vulnerability and death.** Direct creations are the hardest to fully kill. Derived beings can be ended by ordinary cosmic-or-mortal action. (The Vorothar-cannot-be-killed-by-mortal-might rule and the gods-can-die-by-being-forgotten rule both rest on this distinction.)

4. **Authority claims.** Mortal traditions that claim authority directly from a Creator-creation (a mage-order founded by a god; a kingdom founded by a hero whose star elevated to demi-godhood) have stronger cosmic standing than traditions claiming derived authority.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Direct Creator-creations are exactly: `Aru`/`Loran`; `Solaryth`, `Nyxorys`; the seven Seravel; the eight Vatharen; the five pantheon-leader gods; the fourteen Vorothar; the four Firsts (humanic Adam/Eve, demonic Adam/Eve); plus Creator-extension instances of mortal-lineage-anchor stock outside Eden.
- All other beings are derived.
- New direct Creator-creations cannot be silently introduced; canonization requires explicit framework revision.
- Derived beings inherit dilute or composite mana-flavor from their progenitor.
- Mortal mythic exaggeration that claims direct-creation status for derived beings is folk-conflation, not canon.

## Canon Boundaries

- The named cohort of pantheon-leader gods is gated to per-continent canon and the naming of the five pantheons.
- Whether some apparent rare exceptions (pantheon-leader-emanations of unusual ontological status; rare hybrid offspring that gain Vorothar-class scale; very ancient demi-gods whose origin has been forgotten) are properly classified as direct or derived is open canon and may require per-case judgment.
- The relationship between this hierarchy and continent-local mythic tradition is left open: regional traditions may emphasize or de-emphasize the direct/derived distinction in their teaching, but the canonical fact is as locked here.
