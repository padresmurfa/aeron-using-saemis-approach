# Mana

`Mana` is the **fundamental substance** flowing through the entire mythic stack of Aeron. It is the **lifeblood of the Creator** — the cosmic medium by which the Creator's expressed will reaches every tier from [Aru/Loran](../entities/aru.md) down to the smallest mortal life. Every Creator-created being has its own **mana-flavor**: a unique signature of how its mana expresses itself. Every derived being inherits a dilute mana-flavor from its progenitor. Every mortal magical practice is mortal use of mana.

Primary references:
- [Mythic Hierarchy](mythic_hierarchy.md) — tier framework spine
- [Aru](../entities/aru.md), [Loran](../entities/loran.md) — Creator
- [Vatha](../../primal_language/high_abstractions/vatha.md) — Creator's expressed will

## Doctrine

Mana is not a magical resource of mortal stock. Mana is what the cosmic stack is **made of**. To say "the moon `Serenel` has lucid-composure mana-flavor" is to say that the substance of `Serenel`'s being is the Creator's lifeblood expressing itself in the particular pattern of lucid composure. To say "a mortal mage works with `Tavrekal`'s mana" is to say that the mage has cultivated a discipline that allows them to channel a small, attenuated portion of the substance the Ash Moon is composed of.

Mana flows. It runs through the tiers transversely — from Creator down through the moons, the celestials, the gods, the beasts, the mortals — and it does not stop at tier boundaries. A mortal who works with `Velkoraen`'s mana is, in cosmic-substance terms, working with the same substance as `Velkoraen` itself, only at a much-attenuated intensity.

## Mana-Flavors

Every direct Creator-creation has a **unique mana-flavor**. This is a lock-canon rule: there is one Creator's lifeblood, but its expression takes one distinct character per direct creation. Catalog so far:

- **Tier 1 (Creator)** — `Aru`/`Loran` carries all mana as source. The Creator's mana is not "a flavor" so much as the unflavored ground from which all flavors are differentiated.
- **Luminaries** — [`Solaryth`](../astrology/luminaries.md) carries light-vitality mana-flavor; [`Nyxorys`](../astrology/luminaries.md) carries darklight lawful-counter-principle mana-flavor.
- **Tier 2 (Seravel)** — each of the seven moons carries a unique flavor (see [Moons of Aeron](../astrology/moons_of_aeron.md)): Serenel lucid composure; Seramor grave depth; Tavrekal tempering pressure; Taviril distance-made-intimate; Kethorel oath-bound endurance; Oraemor liminal invitation; Serathil measured concealment.
- **Tier 3 (Vatharen)** — each of the eight Vatharen carries a unique flavor (see [Celestials](../astrology/celestials.md)): Vaelvor forward-flow; Vaelmor held-past; Taelden bound-rule; Taelmoth rule-friction; Yirven fecund-unfolding; Yirmor slow-decline; Aenel knotted-link; Aenor distinction-cut.
- **Tier 4 (Pantheon-leader gods)** — each of the five canonical pantheon-leaders carries a unique mana-flavor; this is open canon to be set when individual pantheons are named.
- **Tier 5 (Vorothar)** — each of the fourteen `Vorothar` carries a unique flavor (see individual canon pages in [legendary_beasts](../legendary_beasts/README.md)).

Derived beings (lesser gods, Vorothar-descended kin, mortals descended from firsts) carry **dilute mana-flavor** from their progenitor. A dragon born of `Velkoraen`'s line carries dilute Velkoraen-flavor. A child of two pantheon-gods carries a mixed-flavor inheritance.

## Mana-Flow Through the Stack

Mana flows transversely; it does not respect tier boundaries. A mortal mage attuned to a particular tier-3 Vatharen is in cosmic-substance contact with the Vatharen, and through it (transitively) with the Creator above and (mediated) with the related lesser kin and mortals below.

The flow is **not equal in all directions**. Some flow patterns are more natural than others:

- **Top-down flow** is the canonical direction. The Creator's lifeblood flows downward through the stack into mortals, sustaining the cosmos.
- **Bottom-up flow** is real but much smaller in volume. Mortal prayer, ritual, and worship send small back-currents up the stack — most visibly in faith-brightness modulating tier-4 visibility.
- **Lateral flow** between tier-mates exists but is usually mediated by a higher-tier being.

## Mortal Use of Mana

All mortal magic, ritual, prayer, and divination is **mortal use of mana**. Mortal practice is divided by the source-tier from which the practice draws:

- **Moon-mages** draw from a Seravel.
- **Celestial-mages** ([Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md)-attuned) draw from a Vatharen.
- **Beast-mages** draw from a Vorothar lineage.
- **Priests and god-petitioners** draw from a tier-4 god.
- **Hero-mages** draw from their own Class 2 fated-being star.
- **Place-mages** (druids, geomancers) draw from `Athravenel`'s line or from local genius-loci.
- **Authentication-mages, hunter-mages, etc.** draw from highly-specialized cross-source patterns.

A mortal mage may attune to multiple sources, but each attunement requires sustained discipline. Most mortal mages specialize in one or two source-tiers; rare polymath mages attune to more.

## Mana-Flavor Cross-Effects (Conjunction)

When two mana-flavors are simultaneously active in the same place, they may:

- **Reinforce** (the flavors are aligned; the combined effect is greater than either alone).
- **Cancel** (the flavors are opposed in their cosmic role; the combined effect is less than either alone).
- **Compound** (the flavors combine into a third effect not predictable from either alone — the principle that allows hybrid Vorothar offspring to exist).
- **Mute** (one flavor is suppressed by the other without producing a new effect; rare).

The rules of cross-effect are continent-local in detail but framework-universal in pattern. Specific cross-effects (e.g., `Velkoraen` × `Aelvorath` as storm-wyrms) are noted in individual Vorothar canon entries.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Mana is the substance of the cosmic stack, not a separate magical resource.
- Mana is the Creator's lifeblood. There is one source.
- Every direct Creator-creation has a unique mana-flavor.
- Derived beings inherit dilute mana-flavor from their progenitor.
- Mortal magic is mortal use of mana, not a separate substance.
- Mana flows top-down primarily, bottom-up by faith and prayer (smaller in volume), and laterally with mediation.

## Canon Boundaries

- The detailed mana-flavor of each tier-4 pantheon-leader god is gated to follow the naming and population of pantheons.
- Numeric mana-magnitudes, conversion rates between mana-flavors, and the precise thresholds of mortal attunement are not canonized; these are matters of in-world mage-craft tradition rather than universal physical law.
- The relationship between mana and the physical-energy substrate of [Aeron's planetary frame](aeron.md) is open research-frontier; canonically, mana is **distinct from** physical energy (the world's geology runs on physical law, and the cosmic stack runs on mana), but their interaction surfaces in places like the gaze-resolution mechanic and Vorothar dormancy.
- Whether mana can be "exhausted" in any cosmic sense is canonically held to be no — the Creator's lifeblood does not run out — but mortal attunement-channels can be exhausted, and individual mage-orders treat their access as a finite resource.
