# Light/Darklight Affinity Axis

The **light/darklight affinity axis** is a cosmic principle that propagates through every tier of Aeron's mythic hierarchy. It is **not** a moral axis. It is not a good/evil split. It is a metaphysical-affinity gradient between the mana-character of [Solaryth](../astrology/luminaries.md) (light, vitality, affirmation) and that of [Nyxorys](../astrology/luminaries.md) (darklight, lawful counter-principle). Most cosmic beings have an affinity along this axis; most mortal races inherit a position on it; most cosmic events read against it.

## Doctrine

`Solaryth` carries the mana-flavor of **light**: vitality, growth, affirmation of being. `Nyxorys` carries the mana-flavor of **darklight**: a real metaphysical force that is the lawful counter-principle to light, not its absence. The two are in **permanent diametric opposition** in Aeron's geocentric sky and have been since the world's planetary formation.

Their opposition is **co-essential**, after the manner of [Aru/Loran](../entities/loran.md), [Serenel/Seramor](../astrology/moons_of_aeron.md), and the polar-pair Vatharen at tier 3. Neither side is morally superior; neither side is dispensable; neither side is the original and the other a derivative. Both are direct Creator-creations.

The affinity axis is the gradient between the two. Where any cosmic being or mortal stands on this gradient is their **light/darklight affinity**.

## Propagation Through Tiers

The affinity axis propagates through the cosmic stack:

- **Tier 1 (Creator).** The Creator is **above the axis**. `Aru`/`Loran` is the source of both light and darklight; the Creator does not have a position on the gradient.
- **Luminaries.** [`Solaryth`](../astrology/luminaries.md) is the canonical light-pole. [`Nyxorys`](../astrology/luminaries.md) is the canonical darklight-pole.
- **Tier 2 (Seravel).** The seven moons each have **partial affinity character**, varying by moon. Some moons (e.g., `Serenel`) lean toward light through their public-rhythm and visibility character; others (e.g., `Serathil`, `Oraemor`) sit closer to the darklight side via their concealment and threshold character. The moon mana-flavors are not strictly affinity-determined, but each carries an affinity-flavor as one of its many dimensions.
- **Tier 3 (Vatharen).** The eight Vatharen have varying affinity. Pairs that map onto light/darklight inheritance: `Yirven` (Increase) leans light; `Yirmor` (Decay) leans darklight; `Aenel` (Binding) leans somewhere between, often light-aligned in mortal use; `Aenor` (Severance) leans somewhere between, often darklight-aligned in mortal use. Time-Memory and Order-Strife pairs are largely **axis-neutral** — their cosmic role does not align cleanly to light/darklight.
- **Tier 4 (Pantheons and gods).** Pantheons may be predominantly light-aligned, predominantly darklight-aligned, or mixed. Individual gods have their own position. Continent-local mythic tradition determines the affinity of named pantheons.
- **Tier 5 (Vorothar).** The Vorothar are largely **affinity-mixed** as cataclysmic personalities, but some lean: `Vorthalen` (Hellspawn-Sire) is darklight-aligned in the cosmic-substance sense (fire-and-torment is darklight-mana-coded); `Aelvorath` (Stormwing) tends light-aligned via its sky-aspect; `Athravenel` (Wild-Heart) is largely affinity-neutral (place is not naturally light or darklight); etc.
- **Tier 6 (Mortals and below).** Mortal races have a **structural affinity inheritance** from the [Firsts](../entities/firsts.md): humanic from `Solaryth`-aspected light-affinity Adam/Eve, demonic from `Nyxorys`-aspected darklight-affinity Adam/Eve. Individual mortals can sit anywhere along the alignment-bias gradient (humanic biased chaotic; demonic biased lawful), but the affinity itself is racially-structural.

## Race-Level Affinity at Tier 6

The structural rule for tier-6 mortal races:

- **Humanic mortals** carry **light-affinity** by inheritance from humanic Adam and Eve. They are biased toward **chaotic** alignment as a gradient — not absolute. Individual humanics range across the alignment spectrum but the racial pull is chaotic.
- **Demonic mortals** carry **darklight-affinity** by inheritance from demonic Adam and Eve. They are biased toward **lawful** alignment as a gradient — not absolute. Individual demonics range across the alignment spectrum but the racial pull is lawful.

**The light/darklight axis is not the same as the chaotic/lawful axis.** They run in adjacent dimensions:

- **Light/darklight** is the **metaphysical-mana** axis (Solaryth-Nyxorys).
- **Chaotic/lawful** is the **alignment-bias** axis (D&D-style, but as gradient rather than as discrete categories).

The two axes happen to **correlate** in the canonical inheritance: humanic mortals are light-and-chaotic-biased; demonic mortals are darklight-and-lawful-biased. But the correlation is not strict at the individual level. A humanic mortal can be lawful-biased; a demonic mortal can be chaotic-biased. The racial pull is a gradient probability, not a determinist mandate.

## Not a Moral Axis

The affinity axis is **not moral**. This bears emphasis because mortal traditions sometimes confuse it for a good/evil split:

- A light-affinity being can be morally evil (a tyrannical humanic king; a vain `Solaryth`-aspected mage; an oppressive light-aligned god).
- A darklight-affinity being can be morally good (a wise demonic philosopher; a compassionate `Nyxorys`-aspected priest; a just darklight-aligned god).
- Moral evaluation is **independent** of the affinity axis.

The axis does carry **stylistic and aesthetic** valences in mortal civilization (light-affinity associated with public vitality; darklight-affinity associated with private depth; etc.), but these are cultural rather than ethical.

## Affinity Cross-Currents

Affinity does not segregate cosmic beings or mortals. Cross-affinity interactions are canonical:

- **Mortal cross-marriage** between humanic and demonic occurs and is canonical. Children of mixed unions inherit a mixed affinity-position; their children may stabilize toward one pole or remain mixed.
- **Mage attunement** is not affinity-locked. A humanic mage can attune to a darklight-aligned tier-3 Vatharen; a demonic priest can serve a light-aligned tier-4 god. The attunement-discipline is independent of the mage's racial affinity.
- **Cult and worship traditions** typically span the axis: most pantheons have both light-aligned and darklight-aligned gods within them, and worshippers of either affinity can serve.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Light/darklight is an affinity axis, not a moral axis.
- It propagates through every tier of the cosmic stack.
- The Creator (`Aru`/`Loran`) is **above** the axis.
- Both poles (`Solaryth` and `Nyxorys`) are direct Creator-creations and co-essential.
- Humanic mortals are light-affinity (and chaotic-biased); demonic mortals are darklight-affinity (and lawful-biased). Both racial inheritances trace to the [Firsts](../entities/firsts.md).
- The affinity axis is **not** the same as the chaotic/lawful alignment axis, though they correlate at the racial level.
- Cross-affinity interactions (marriage, mage-attunement, worship) are canonical.

## Canon Boundaries

- The exact mana-flavor character of `Solaryth` and `Nyxorys` is canonized in [luminaries.md](../astrology/luminaries.md); affinity character of individual moons, Vatharen, gods, and beasts is sketched here but elaborated per-entity in their own canon pages.
- The mortal-civic consequences of affinity (does a kingdom legally mark its citizens by affinity? do trade-routes follow affinity-lines?) are continent-local.
- The metaphysics of mixed-affinity offspring across multiple generations is open and likely a research-frontier in mortal lineage-magic.
- The interaction between the light/darklight affinity axis and the [chaotic/lawful alignment-bias gradient](../entities/firsts.md) is canonized as "correlated but independent at the individual level"; the in-world philosophy of how that works is open.
