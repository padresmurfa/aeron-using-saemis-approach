# Mythopedia

`mythopedia` is the organized reference layer of Aeron.

## Use
- Record canonical facts, entities, events, places, laws, and relationships.
- Prefer one topic per file.
- Write for lookup and consistency, not for dramatic effect.
- When a concept has a primal-language headword, introduce the primal term and English gloss together. After that, prefer the primal word alone or `PrimalWord (English gloss)` instead of letting the English gloss stand alone.

## Mythic-Hierarchy Spine

The unifying canon for Aeron's mythic stack is anchored at:
- `cosmology/mythic_hierarchy.md` — the tier framework spine (Creator → Moons → Celestials → Pantheons → Constellations → Mortals)
- `cosmology/mana.md` — mana doctrine (the substance flowing through the entire stack)
- `cosmology/light_darklight_affinity.md` — the affinity axis as it propagates through every tier
- `cosmology/creation_hierarchy.md` — direct vs derived creation enumeration
- `astrology/celestials.md` — tier 3 (eight Vatharen)
- `cosmology/pantheons.md` — tier 4 (five canonical pantheons)
- `legendary_beasts/README.md` — tier 5 (fourteen Vorothar plus descended kin)
- `cosmology/mortal_tier.md` — tier 6 (mortal slot inventory)
- `astrology/star_fate.md` — R2 stars doctrine with positional weighting
- `mechanics/hero_summoning.md` — hero-summoning, reincarnation, and prophecy
- `entities/firsts.md` — humanic and demonic Adam/Eve
- `places/eden.md` — the unreachable garden where the Firsts dwell

## Current Structure
- `astrology/` for luminaries, stars, constellations, moons, celestial canon, calendars, and omen-bearing sky events
- `cosmology/` for states, laws, structural metaphysics, world-scale continuity models, the mythic hierarchy spine, and tier-spanning doctrines
- `entities/` for beings and named intelligences (Creator, Firsts, future named gods)
- `events/` for canonical threshold acts and major happenings
- `legendary_beasts/` for tier-5 cataclysmic-beast canon (Vorothar and descended-kin classes)
- `mechanics/` for canonical cosmic mechanics (hero-summoning, reincarnation, prophecy, and future ritual-mechanics)
- `places/` for named locations of canonical significance (Eden; future continent canon)
- `timeline/` for ordered era-by-era chronology

## Relationship To Other Folders
- `astrology/` gathers sky canon where creation-era cosmology meets planet-era observation, calendars, and omen doctrine.
- `legendary_beasts/` and `cosmology/` together hold the mythic-hierarchy spine and per-tier canon.
- `sagas/` may dramatize or narrate events.
- `timeline/` indexes canon across the reference and saga layers.
- `mythopedia/` resolves what is canon when narrative wording is ambiguous.
