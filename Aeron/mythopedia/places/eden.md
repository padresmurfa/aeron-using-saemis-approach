# Eden

`Eden` is the hidden garden on Aeron in which the four firsts — humanic Adam, humanic Eve, demonic Adam, demonic Eve — dwell in blessed eternal life, unreachable except by the Creator. It is **on Aeron**, not above it; it is **a place**, not a metaphor; and it is **canonically real but canonically inaccessible to mortal exploration**.

## Doctrine

`Eden` is the place the Creator made for the four firsts. It is a garden in the broad sense — not necessarily a literal garden of plants, although mortal traditions tend to render it that way — and it is the dwelling-place of beings whom the Creator made to live blessed and undying.

`Eden` is on Aeron in the cosmic-spatial sense. It occupies a geographical location somewhere on the world's surface or within its layered structure. But the location is not findable by mortal means: travelers who set out toward where Eden is rumored to be do not arrive there. Mages who scry for it find their scrying defeated. Geographers who triangulate its position find their triangulations impossible to reproduce. The unreachability is not magical concealment in the sense of a strong illusion; it is **structural to Eden's nature** — the place is at a distance from mortal access that does not correspond to mortal-measurable distance.

The Creator visits `Eden`. The firsts have not left it. No mortal of any descended generation has been there.

## Inhabitants

`Eden` is inhabited by exactly four beings:

- **Humanic Adam** — the first humanic, directly Creator-created, light-affinity gradient (chaotic-biased).
- **Humanic Eve** — paired with humanic Adam, directly Creator-created, light-affinity.
- **Demonic Adam** — the first demonic, directly Creator-created, darklight-affinity gradient (lawful-biased).
- **Demonic Eve** — paired with demonic Adam, directly Creator-created, darklight-affinity.

The four firsts coexist in `Eden` without conflict. Humanic and demonic are not moral opposites (see [Light/Darklight Affinity](../cosmology/light_darklight_affinity.md)); their proximity in `Eden` is canonical and harmonious.

The firsts are blessed with eternal life. They have not fallen, in any sense — there is no canonical "fall of the firsts" doctrine in Aeron. The mortal lineages that descend from them did not descend by being expelled; they descend by Creator-extension into the ordinary world (see [Adam and Eve canon](../entities/firsts.md)).

## Function in Cosmology

`Eden` serves three cosmological functions:

1. **The dwelling-place of the firsts.** It is where the four direct Creator-creations of mortal-tier kin remain. Without `Eden`, the firsts would have to live among ordinary mortals, and their direct-creation status would conflict with mortal mortality, mortal alignment, and mortal cosmic-significance.

2. **The cosmic origin-anchor of mortal mana.** Every humanic mortal carries a dilute mana-flavor traceable, through generations, to the humanic firsts in `Eden`. The same for every demonic mortal. `Eden` is the living source from which mortal mana descends.

3. **The Creator's continued direct mortal-tier presence.** Above tier 6, the Creator's expressed will runs through Vatharen, gods, beasts, and so on — many intermediate steps. At tier 6, the Creator's direct creation persists in `Eden`. This is the cosmic ground of mortal-Creator relation: the Creator has mortal-tier creatures of His own making, still alive, still in direct relation with Him.

## Why `Eden` is Unreachable

`Eden` is unreachable to mortals because **mortal access would dissolve its function**. If mortals could reach the firsts, the firsts would become a population with location, with politics, with mortal interaction, with possibly-violable status. The Creator's direct mortal-tier creations would no longer be set apart from descended mortal life. The cosmic anchor would fail.

The canonical answer to "why can't I find Eden?" in mortal mythic tradition is therefore: **because if you could, it would not be Eden anymore.**

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- `Eden` is on Aeron, not above it or beyond it.
- `Eden` is a real place with a real geographical location.
- `Eden` is unreachable to mortals (and to descended gods, beasts, Vatharen, and other tier-above beings; only the Creator may visit).
- `Eden` is inhabited by exactly four beings — humanic Adam, humanic Eve, demonic Adam, demonic Eve — all directly Creator-created, all blessed with eternal life, none fallen.
- The four firsts have not left `Eden`. Mortal lineages descend from Creator-extensions into the ordinary world, not from expulsions of the firsts.
- The unreachability is structural, not enforced by any specific magic, ward, or mortal-policed boundary.

## Canon Boundaries

- The exact geographical location of `Eden` on Aeron is canonically held to be unknown and likely unknowable.
- The visual character of `Eden` — whether it is literally a garden, a city, a mountain, a forest, an island, an underground cavern, or some combination — is open canon. Mortal mythic traditions vary widely.
- The internal life of `Eden` (what the firsts do day-to-day; how they pass eternity; whether they are aware of their descended mortal lineages) is open and may be set in narrative work.
- Whether the Creator visits `Eden` regularly, occasionally, or only at tier-defining cosmic moments is open.
- Whether `Eden` interacts with other tier entities (do the firsts see the same sky as ordinary mortals? do the moons cycle there? is the gaze-resolution mechanic operative there?) is open and likely will be left open.
- Whether anything ever leaves `Eden` outbound (a message from a first to a descended mortal? a reply to mortal prayer that is somehow specifically Eden-sourced?) is open.
