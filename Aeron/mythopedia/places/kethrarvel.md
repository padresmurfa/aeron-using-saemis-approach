# Kethrarvel — The Frostbound North

`Kethrarvel` is the northern continent of Aeron — frostbound, fortified, and oath-bound. Its primal-language headword is [Kethrarvel](../../primal_language/structural_principles/kethrarvel.md) (Keth- enclosure/oath/iron family + -arvel frost-northern).

## Geographical Character

`Kethrarvel` spans high northern latitudes. The continent is dominated by long winter; brief summer; tundra, taiga, and ice-shelf coastlines; great frozen rivers that flow only in the warm months. Mountain ranges in the interior shelter the deep places where the dwelling Vorothar coil and lie. Glaciers and ice-fields are extensive; mortal civilization clings to the temperate fringes and the ice-protected fjords.

The local sky over `Kethrarvel` is canonically among the most dramatic on Aeron — long polar-aspect nights bring extended visibility of the stars and the dwelling Vorothar's constellations; the [Wyrm-Sire's coil-pattern](../legendary_beasts/velkoraen.md) and the [Wraith-Sovereign's tomb-throne pattern](../legendary_beasts/skaelmorath.md) are bright over the continent for much of the year.

## Cultural Civilizational Families

`Kethrarvel`'s mortal civilization is **fortified, oath-bound, and dynastic**. Sketched character:

- Mortal kingdoms here have long unbroken dynastic lines; constitutional sovereignty traditions trace founding-myths to encounters with [Skaelmorath](../legendary_beasts/skaelmorath.md)'s line.
- Iron-craft, fortification, and the keeping of long oaths are central crafts; the [Kethorel](../astrology/moons_of_aeron.md) (Iron Moon) is canonically venerated above other moons.
- The mortal traditions canonically retain memory of cosmic-scale histories; the Wraith-Sovereign's continental presence shapes mortal-civic constitutional thought.

## Hosted Vorothar

Two Vorothar dwell on `Kethrarvel`:

- **[Skaelmorath](../legendary_beasts/skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign)** — dormant in a great frozen mausoleum-throne complex deep within the continent's interior, beneath ice and stone. The dwelling-site is folk-known and is a canonical pilgrim-site for mortal sovereignty traditions.
- **[Velkoraen](../legendary_beasts/velkoraen.md) (Wyrm-Sire)** — coiled within a great ice-mountain, the body extending through deep cold passages no mortal has fully mapped. Local mortal civilizations have the longest-running treasure-and-hoard traditions on Aeron, drawn from `Velkoraen`'s wyrm-mana.

## Pantheon Affiliation

Primary pantheon-affiliation on `Kethrarvel` is **deferred to per-pantheon canon**. The continent canonically hosts at least one primary pantheon worship-tradition with strong oath-and-iron emphasis.

## Mythic Role

`Kethrarvel` is, in Aeron's folk-memory, the cosmic patron-region for **constitutional sovereignty, lasting oath, and the long survival of dynasties under hard winter**. Cross-continent mortal travelers seeking ancient oaths, dynastic counsel, or formal constitutional authority canonically pilgrim to `Kethrarvel`.

The Wraith-Sovereign's mausoleum-throne is a tier-defining cosmic site; mortal sovereigns of other continents canonically acknowledge cosmic precedent in `Skaelmorath`'s presence on `Kethrarvel`.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- `Kethrarvel` is the frostbound northern continent.
- It hosts [Skaelmorath](../legendary_beasts/skaelmorath.md) and [Velkoraen](../legendary_beasts/velkoraen.md).
- It is climatically harsh but inhabited, with continuous mortal civilization.
- The cosmic resonance of the [Kethorel](../astrology/moons_of_aeron.md) Iron Moon is canonically strongest here.

## Canon Boundaries

- Specific named northern kingdoms, dynasties, and frost-mage traditions are downstream.
- The exact location of `Skaelmorath`'s mausoleum-throne site is folk-known but not narrow-mapped here.
- The pantheon primary-affiliation is open and downstream.
- The mortal taxonomy of frost-aspected lesser kin (frost-wraiths, ice-wyrms, frost-giants if they descend through Drathalon-cross lineages) is open and downstream.
