# Continents (Athralin)

`Aeron` is divided into **seven canonical continents** — the [`Athralin`](../../primal_language/structural_principles/athralin.md). They are the great landmasses upon which mortal civilization, [Vorothar](../legendary_beasts/README.md) dwellings, and [pantheon-worship traditions](../cosmology/pantheons.md) are arrayed.

This page is the canon spine for Aeron's continent inventory. Per-continent canon entries are linked below.

## Doctrine

The seven `Athralin` are universal as places — every continent exists for every Aeronian observer; the same seven continents are present everywhere — and continent-local in their inhabited content. Different continents host different mortal races, different kingdoms, different mage-orders, different mythic traditions, and different primary [pantheon](../cosmology/pantheons.md) affiliations.

Each continent hosts **two** [Vorothar](../legendary_beasts/README.md). The continent-Vorothar pairings are canon and below.

A continent is **not equivalent** to a tectonic plate. Continent canon is a mythopedia concern (named, mythic landforms); tectonic-plate inventory is a planetary-physics concern (`cosmology/aeron.md`). The two layers may not align cleanly; a single continent may span multiple plates, or a single plate may host parts of multiple continents.

## Vorothar Dwellings

Each continent hosts two Vorothar. The constellation of each beast shines brightest over its dwelling continent (per the [tier-5 doctrine](../legendary_beasts/README.md)).

| `Kethrarvel` | [`Skaelmorath`](../legendary_beasts/skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign, frozen tomb-throne) | [`Velkoraen`](../legendary_beasts/velkoraen.md) (Wyrm-Sire, in deep ice-mountain coil) |

| `Phaeravar` | [`Vothorisk`](../legendary_beasts/vothorisk.md) (Eldritch-Eye, dwelling that does not stably map) | [`Phaelvenor`](../legendary_beasts/phaelvenor.md) (Swarm-Voice, on the steppes) |
| `Seralinor` | [`Drovenrath`](../legendary_beasts/drovenrath.md) (Leviathan-Deep, in the abyssal trench) | [`Krevoraen`](../legendary_beasts/krevoraen.md) (Bloodborn-Sire, an old island-castle) |
| `Tavorath` | [`Mirathven`](../legendary_beasts/mirathven.md) (Chimera-Mother, in the labyrinth of high passes) | [`Vesselrath`](../legendary_beasts/vesselrath.md) (Plague-Mother, in the cloud-marsh plateau) |

The `Oraevenel`/`Athravenel` pairing is canonically singular: `Athravenel`'s body **is** the continent. The other Vorothar dwelling on `Oraevenel` (`Khaethorom`) inhabits the half-mappable verges within Athravenel's territorial body.

## Pantheon-to-Continent Affiliation

Pantheon primary-affiliation per continent is **deferred** until the five pantheons are named (downstream canon). Each continent will canonically host one or more primary pantheon-worship traditions, with cross-continent overlap and syncretism allowed.

## Universal-vs-Positional at the Continent Layer

Continents are universal as places — they all exist on the same Aeron — and positional in observed sky. A traveler on `Tavorath` sees a different sky-bright pattern from a traveler on `Athralorn` simultaneously, because:

- The dwelling Vorothar's constellation is bright over its continent and dim elsewhere.
- The galaxies of the [pantheons](../cosmology/pantheons.md) shine over different regions at different hours.
- The [Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md) wandering force-bearers are visible from different continents at different hours of the geocentric-round rotation.

This is not a Earth-style sky-difference of latitude; it is a layered tier-5/tier-4/tier-3 visibility-positionality compounded with geocentric-round geometry.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Aeron has **exactly seven** canonical continents. There are not six; there are not eight.
- Each continent has a primal-language name from the locked inventory above.
- Each continent hosts exactly two [Vorothar](../legendary_beasts/README.md), per the locked dwelling table.
- The `Oraevenel`/`Athravenel` "the continent IS her body" relation is canonical and singular.
- A continent is not equivalent to a tectonic plate.
- Continents are universal as places, continent-local in inhabited content.

## Canon Boundaries

- Pantheon primary-affiliation per continent is open and deferred.
- Specific kingdoms, named mage-orders, mortal-civic geography, and detailed climate maps within each continent are open and downstream per-continent canon.
- The exact tectonic-plate-to-continent mapping is open and is a planetary-physics concern.
- The Aeron-spheroid celestial placement of Vorothar constellations and pantheon galaxies relative to each continent is open canon, gated to the named celestial-sphere mapping.
- Cross-continent migration history of mortal races is open and continent-local.
- Continent-spanning beast-lineages (lesser kin that migrate between continents) are open canon.
