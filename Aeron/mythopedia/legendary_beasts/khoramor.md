# Khoramor — The Maw-Eternal

`Khoramor`, the **Maw-Eternal**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose form is appetite given body. The progenitor of every world-eater, tarrasque, glutton-cataclysm, and behir-class hunger-spirit too immense to be a hunger of any particular thing.

## Aspect and Domain

`Khoramor` is the cosmic principle of incarnate hunger. Not metabolic hunger (a need to eat to live); appetitive hunger (the eating that does not need a reason). The descended kin do not consume because they need; they consume because consumption is what they are. A tarrasque crossing a continent does not stop when satiated, because satiation is not in the lineage. The mouth is the central feature; the stomach is the organ that defines the form.

## Visual Form

`Khoramor` is recorded variously by mortal accounts but consistently described as **mostly mouth**. The body is supportive infrastructure for an enormous central maw lined with multiple rows of teeth. Limbs may be vestigial or massive, depending on which kin-lineage the witness has unconsciously interpolated, but the mouth is always the canonical center. The throat is dark in a way that swallows light; some accounts say a viewer at peak attunement can see down the throat and find that it does not end.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Khoramor` mana-flavor is **the pull of consumption**. It feels like the gravity of an empty stomach scaled to cosmic substance. Mortals near a `Khoramor`-kin lineage often experience appetite displacement: hunger when they should not be hungry, the urge to consume what is not nourishment. Mages who attune to `Khoramor` (uncommon; the discipline is destabilizing to the mage's own hunger-cycle) wield this as a force of erasure: their work consumes rather than transforms.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Maw-Eternal's constellation sees `Khoramor` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern. The viewer sees mostly the inside of the throat — perspective is inevitably oriented toward the mouth's interior. The throat is bottomless. `Khoramor` is **aware** of the viewer; the awareness manifests as the felt experience of being noticed by the mouth specifically. The reply, when it comes, is the felt offer of being consumed. Mortals who survive this gaze do not usually pray to `Khoramor` again.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Khoramor` is aware of every devourer-kin descendant and of every mortal who has been consumed by them — the consumed are remembered in `Khoramor`'s line as cosmic-scale satiation. Prayer to `Khoramor` is a fringe practice, undertaken mostly by those who would invoke the Maw to consume something the petitioner cannot remove themselves: a curse, a disease, a memory, occasionally another mortal. The cost of such prayer is canonically high; `Khoramor` does not consume on credit.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Khoramor` dwells dormant in a great cleft or cavern at the heart of [Miravarath](../places/miravarath.md), the Burning South — a place that mortals know to avoid because animals do not return from it and the wind makes a low constant sound that mortals hear as breathing. The dwelling-region is canonically barren. Plants grow elsewhere on Miravarath. Within the cleft, even moss does not.

## Stirring

`Khoramor` stirs:
- during long famines (the cosmic principle is mirror-stirred by mortal hunger)
- when mortal civilization commits acts of mass consumption (cities consumed by their own waste, oceans drained for industry, forests entirely cleared)
- when descended devourer-kin numbers fall below cosmic-mythic thresholds (`Khoramor` will birth replacements; the stirring is restorative)
- when the multi-millennial mythic cycle renews

A stirred `Khoramor` is regional-apocalyptic: the mouth crosses landscapes consuming mass-scale targets. The defeat of a stirred `Khoramor` requires the cooperation of multiple `Vorothar`-aligned forces, and even then the canonical defeat is "re-dormant," not "killed."

## Descended Kin

The devourer-kin lineage includes: tarrasques (continent-crossing, armored, mortal-hunting), world-eaters (oceanic, slow, mass-destroying), gluttons-of-cosmic-scale (regional cataclysm-scale gluttony), behir-class (multi-segmented, lightning-throated), grub-cataclysms (juvenile-form world-eaters that mature into adults), and the regional named devourer-cataclysms across continents.

Hybrid offspring: `Khoramor` × `Velkoraen` produces wyrm-mawed devourer-class (the "all-mouth dragons" of folk-memory); `Khoramor` × `Drovenrath` produces deep-trench eaters (rare, pressure-shaped, abyssal); `Khoramor` × `Phaelvenor` produces consuming-swarms (locust-cataclysms with the appetite of Maw-kin scaled into hive-form).

## Mythic Role

`Khoramor` in folk-memory is the warning against consumption-without-end. Civilizations build their granary-rituals, their hoarding-restrictions, and their famine-prayers around the stories of `Khoramor`. The Maw-Eternal is the cosmic answer to the question: what happens if mortal civilization develops appetite without restraint?

## Canon Boundaries

- `Khoramor`'s dwelling continent is open canon.
- The exact form of the body — whether limbs are vestigial or massive, whether the body is bipedal or quadrupedal or otherwise — is intentionally open; folk-memory varies.
- Specific named devourer-kin (named tarrasques, regional world-eater stories) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Khoramor`'s mana-flavor and the [Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md) `Yirmor` (Decay) is open research-frontier; mortal scholars debate whether the two interact at conjunction or are entirely independent.
