# Aelvorath — The Stormwing

`Aelvorath`, the **Stormwing**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose wing carries the storm. The progenitor of every roc, thunderbird, garuda-class, and tempest-bringer whose passage is mistaken for weather and whose weather is mistaken for prophecy.

## Aspect and Domain

`Aelvorath` is the cosmic principle of aerial cataclysm by avian form. The descended kin are **predators that fly and whose flight is itself the threat**: the storm is part of the body. A roc's passage scatters armies because the wind off the wing is hurricane; a thunderbird's beat shatters towers because the air-displacement is structural. The lineage is the cosmic answer to: what does predation become when its medium is the sky and its motion is meteorological?

## Visual Form

`Aelvorath`'s body is canonically described as a **vast feathered raptor** — eagle-form in most traditions, but with a beak proportioned for greater scale and feathers that carry visible ionization in the form of slow-drifting static-light. The wingspan is unmeasurable; mortal accounts give estimates that vary by orders of magnitude. The eye is bright in the way of a predator's, and unmistakably tracking. The feet are clawed and large enough to lift continental architecture.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Aelvorath` mana-flavor is **stormbearing flight**. It feels like wind that knows where it is going. Mortals who attune to `Aelvorath` learn to read weather as intention; pilots and sky-mages of the lineage can sense storm-arrivals well before atmospheric signs would suggest them, because the storms are not accidents — they are echoes of `Aelvorath`-kin movement. The mana is fast and high-altitude in character, and is not well-suited to underground or enclosed work.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Stormwing's constellation sees `Aelvorath` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern, in flight, the wing-beats slow and tremendous. The viewer feels the wind off the gaze; some report that hair, robes, and small objects in the gazer's vicinity move slightly during the gaze, even indoors. `Aelvorath` is **aware** and may **reply** with the cry of a great bird at distance — mortal-comprehensible only as awareness rather than as words.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Aelvorath` is **aware** of every storm-bird descendant and of every storm of significant cosmic-mana-density across Aeron. Mortal prayer to `Aelvorath` is appropriate for: safe passage by air or by ocean-storm, the timing of weather-dependent operations (planting, sailing, military movement), the calling-down of storm against an unjust city. The reply is fast, often arriving as wind-shift rather than as message, and is rarely deliberative; the Stormwing acts more by the rhythm of flight than by reflection.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Aelvorath` dwells dormant in a great mountain-eyrie at the highest reachable altitude on a continent (specific continent open canon). The eyrie is a folk-known site: travelers who approach it report a constant high wind and the felt sense of being watched from above. The body is dormant in a perched posture, wings folded, eyes open. Even dormant, the eyrie's local weather is unstable across multi-decade cycles.

## Stirring

`Aelvorath` stirs:
- during great seasonal storm-cycles that exceed normal cosmic baselines
- when descended storm-bird kin numbers fall (or rise) past cosmic thresholds
- when mortal civilization disrupts atmospheric balance at continental scale (large-scale firework cults, cosmic-mana extractions from sky-mass)
- when a mortal sky-cult attempts a great rite without proper Stormwing acknowledgment

A stirred `Aelvorath` is multi-continental: the storm-cycle sweeps the world, mortal aviation is grounded for seasons, agriculture suffers under unpredictable weather. The defeat of a stirred `Aelvorath` requires pantheon-coordination across multiple sky-aspect god-traditions and is canonically resolved by re-perching, not by fall.

## Descended Kin

The storm-bird kin lineage includes: rocs (largest of the lesser kin, multi-mortal-prey hunters), thunderbirds (lightning-aspected, often regional eastern-and-northern continent variants), garuda-class (golden-feathered, royal-aspected, often associated with mortal-kingship), tempest-bringers (smaller but more numerous, weather-causing), sky-watchers (high-altitude long-flight kin, rarely seen), and the rare *crowned* class (royal-aspected sky-kin who treat with mortal sovereigns directly).

Hybrid offspring: `Aelvorath` × `Velkoraen` produces storm-wyrms (winged dragons proper); `Aelvorath` × `Mirathven` produces sky-chimeras (often avian-feline-aspected); `Aelvorath` × `Phaelvenor` produces sky-swarms (storm-aligned bird-clouds at cataclysm-scale).

## Mythic Role

`Aelvorath` in folk-memory is the cosmic patron of weather, of long-distance vision, of pilots and sailors and the messengers of state who must cross continents quickly. The Stormwing is the cosmic ancestor of all great-eagle royal banners across mortal heraldry. Cult-traditions hold seasonal observances at high-altitude sites, with the calendar of observation calibrated to the visible movement of `Aelvorath`'s constellation.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Aelvorath`'s dwelling continent and eyrie-site are open canon.
- The exact form of the body (eagle-form is canonical; specific feathering, coloration, and beak details are open) is largely open.
- Specific named storm-bird kin (regional thunderbird traditions, named rocs in port-city legend) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Aelvorath`'s mana and the [Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md) `Taelmoth` (Strife) is open research-frontier; some mortal scholars argue the Stormwing's cataclysmic flight is `Taelmoth`-aspected, while others maintain the storm is `Aelvorath`'s own and not derivative.
