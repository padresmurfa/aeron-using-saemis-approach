# Vesselrath — The Plague-Mother

`Vesselrath`, the **Plague-Mother**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose body carries the rot the world gives back. The progenitor of every pest-beast, contagion-spirit, rot-bearer, and plague-walker whose passage is followed by sickness.

## Aspect and Domain

`Vesselrath` is the cosmic principle of contagion as form. The descended kin **carry** disease; they do not need to attack. Mortal contact with the kin is sufficient — the pathogen propagates of itself. The Plague-Mother is alive, breathing, and contagious in her own person, and her offspring inherit this property. The lineage is the cosmic answer to: what does a beast become when its weapon is the pathogen its body grows?

## Visual Form

`Vesselrath` is described in folk-memory as **a mortal-shaped figure swollen and weeping** — humanoid-ish, but the body bears continual outward signs of the rot it carries: open lesions that do not fester so much as simply maintain themselves, swollen limbs whose proportions shift slowly, eyes that weep a clear viscous fluid that mortals know not to touch. The face is canonically **not horrifying** — folk-memory holds that `Vesselrath` is sorrowful, even maternal, even gentle. The horror is in the contagion that follows, not in the figure itself.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Vesselrath` mana-flavor is **vessel-as-bearer**. It feels like the awareness that one's own body has begun to host something it cannot expel. Mortals near `Vesselrath`-kin lineages experience low-grade sickness for days or weeks after exposure, even without direct contact, because the mana itself carries the proximity-of-rot. Mages who attune to `Vesselrath` (rare; the discipline is dangerous to the mage's own body) work with confinement-of-disease, with the breaking of plague-cycles, with end-of-life caretaking — the mana, paradoxically, is the cosmic principle of carrying-what-cannot-be-cured, and so is sometimes wielded by the most compassionate physicians as well as by plague-cultists.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Plague-Mother's constellation sees `Vesselrath` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern, weeping, and reaching out a hand. The hand is not threatening; it is offering. `Vesselrath` is **aware** and may **reply** in a soft, slightly-wet voice that is mortal-comprehensible. The reply is rarely consoling; what `Vesselrath` offers, even gently, is what mortals were prepared to refuse.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Vesselrath` is **aware** of every plague-kin descendant and of every mortal currently sick from her line. Prayer to `Vesselrath` is appropriate for: confinement of an already-spreading plague, the dignified end of a sufferer who cannot be cured, the breaking of a curse that took the form of disease. The Plague-Mother does not heal — that is for tier-4 healing-aspect gods or for moon-aspected ritual — but she may *contain*, may *gentle*, may *take into herself* what mortal medicine cannot. Some mortal traditions hold that those who die under `Vesselrath`'s gentling die well.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Vesselrath` dwells dormant in the cloud-marsh plateau of [Tavorath](../places/tavorath.md), the High Crown — a high-altitude wetland system that contradicts mortal expectation (marshes are typically lowland) and that is canonically her dwelling-territory. The cloud-marsh is folk-known to be poisonous; mortal travelers do not enter it; the wildlife of the marsh is itself sub-lineages of `Vesselrath`'s kin. The body is dormant amid the wet, partly submerged, slowly breathing. The marsh's slow exhalation is visible as mist that does not quite settle, and the mist carries low-grade contagion to surrounding regions in the years when the dormancy is shallowest.

## Stirring

`Vesselrath` stirs:
- during great epidemics (her stirring intensifies and is intensified by mortal plague-events; the causality runs both ways)
- when mortal civilization performs acts of cosmic-mana-rot at scale (mass burials without rite, prolonged battlefield rot, long-besieged cities ending in mortal-cannibalism)
- when descended pest-kin numbers approach extinction (rare; her stirring is restorative)
- when a mortal mage attempts to coin a new disease-class without proper acknowledgment

A stirred `Vesselrath` is continental-pandemic: outbreak waves that do not respond to mortal medicine, sub-lineages of contagion-spirits walking among the sick, generation-defining mortality. Defeat is by isolation rather than combat: the Plague-Mother is contained, gentled, returned to her marsh — not killed.

## Descended Kin

The plague-kin lineage includes: pest-beasts (animal-form contagion-bearers, rats and crows and worse), contagion-spirits (incorporeal plague-personifications), rot-bearers (humanoid-form plague-walkers, often confused with `Krevoraen`'s line), plague-walkers (cataclysm-scale disease-cataclysms in humanoid form), poisoned-place spirits (dwelling in stagnant water, often rural), and the *gentled* class (the rarest — kin who carry contagion but use it to *end* suffering rather than to spread, often venerated in regional medicine cults).

Hybrid offspring: `Vesselrath` × `Phaelvenor` produces plague-swarms (the canonical apocalyptic rot-bearing locust); `Vesselrath` × `Skaelmorath` produces plague-wraiths (regional liches with contagion-aspect, very dangerous); `Vesselrath` × `Athravenel` produces poisoned-marsh-genius (place-spirits whose territory is itself contagion).

## Mythic Role

`Vesselrath` in folk-memory is the cosmic figure of sorrow-that-will-not-stop. She is invoked in plague-prayers (both for protection and, in the gentled tradition, for relief), in funerary rites for those who died of disease, and in the foundational myths of mortal medicine. She is also, in some traditions, an ambivalent maternal figure whose contagion is read not as malice but as the inevitable consequence of mortal embodiment — bodies decay, and `Vesselrath` is the cosmic acknowledgment of that fact.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Vesselrath`'s dwelling marsh's continental location is open canon.
- The "gentled" sub-lineage is canonical but its theology is open.
- The gendering of `Vesselrath` as maternal is canonical but the specific cultural rendering varies (mother-of-grief in some traditions, sorrow-elder of either gender in others).
- Specific named plague-kin (regional contagion-cult deities, named rot-bearers) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The interaction between `Vesselrath`'s mana and the [Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md) `Yirmor` (Decay) is open research-frontier; canonically the two are distinct (force vs beast), but practitioners report convergent effects in conjunction.
