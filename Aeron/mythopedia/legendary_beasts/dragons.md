# Dragons (and Beast-Descendant Classes)

This page is the canonical entry-point for the **derived classes** of legendary beasts — kin who descend from one or more [Vorothar](README.md) but are themselves below tier-5 cataclysmic-scale, occupying the lesser-beast space of tier 5 and (in some cases) the upper edge of tier 6 monster.

## Doctrine

Beast-descendant classes are **derived beings, not direct Creator-creations**. They descend from one or more Vorothar via direct generation, aspect-emanation, dream-seeding, or hybrid-cross. They inherit a **dilute mana-flavor** from their progenitor(s). They are aware, communicable, mortal-killable in extremis, and populate the world in their dozens to hundreds.

The most famous of these descendant classes are the **dragons**, descended primarily from [`Velkoraen` (Wyrm-Sire)](velkoraen.md). But dragons are only one of many derived classes; the lesser-kin space of every Vorothar generates its own descendant classes, and hybrid-cross lineages add further variety.

This page sketches the framework for derived classes and provides the canonical entry-point for downstream per-class canon.

## The Dragon Class

### Origin

Dragons descend from [`Velkoraen`](velkoraen.md). Most dragons descend by direct generation across long parental-time-scales, with occasional aspect-emanation events producing new sub-lineages. Some dragons descend from hybrid crosses (storm-wyrms from `Velkoraen` × `Aelvorath`, deep-sea-coilers from `Velkoraen` × `Drovenrath`, fire-wyrms from `Velkoraen` × `Vorthalen`).

### Mana-Flavor

Dragons carry **dilute Velkoraen mana-flavor**: slow ancient depth, hoard-mind, coiled compressed power. Sub-lineages add further flavor from hybrid-cross parents.

### Sub-Lineages

Common dragon sub-lineages include:

- **True-dragons** (proper Velkoraen-descended, winged, breath-aspected, hoarding) — the central class.
- **Wyrms-of-the-deep-rock** (limbless, mineral-aspected, subterranean) — closely-Velkoraen-aligned but with rock-affinity emphasis.
- **Sea-serpents-of-the-coil** (oceanic, distinct from `Drovenrath`'s pressure-shaped deep-kin; the canonical test is hoard-tendency vs lurk-tendency).
- **Hydras** (multi-headed, regenerative; regional folk-memory).
- **Lung** (sky-rivers, fortune-aspected, regional eastern-continent variants).
- **Tatzelwurm** (small-cataclysmic, alpine, regional variant).
- **Storm-wyrms** (winged-dragons proper, `Velkoraen` × `Aelvorath` hybrid).
- **Fire-wyrms** (`Velkoraen` × `Vorthalen` hybrid; often misidentified by mortal scholars as proper dragons).

The full taxonomy of dragon sub-lineages is open canon and continent-local in detail.

### Powers

Dragons inherit (in dilute form):
- **Compressed deferred force** (`Velkoraen` mana-flavor): patience, hoarding, the slow-uncoil-when-released.
- **Breath-aspect** (winged sub-lineages): elemental-aligned flame, frost, lightning, or sub-elemental variants.
- **Long life**: dragons often outlive multiple mortal generations, though they are not deathless.
- **Aware mind**: dragons are intelligent; many speak mortal languages; some keep mortal-style courts of dependents.
- **Hoard-mind**: dragons accumulate physical hoards, often inflected with cosmic-symbolic significance (memory-hoards, treaty-hoards, oath-hoards) rather than purely material.

### Vulnerability

Dragons can be killed. Mortal heroes have killed dragons across folk-memory. The killing usually requires great resources (a hero of cosmic-significance, a relic-weapon, the cooperation of a god-tier or beast-tier being). Dragons are not Vorothar; they are not above mortal force in extremis.

### Mortal-Cult Traditions

Dragon-cult traditions vary widely by continent and by sub-lineage. Some dragons are cosmic-allies of mortal kingdoms; some are adversaries; some are indifferent. Dragon-rider cultures, hoard-mage academies, and dragon-bane hunter-orders all exist.

## Hybrid-Cross Classes

Hybrid offspring across Vorothar lineages are real, canonical, and produce some of the most narratively-significant lesser kin. Notable hybrid-cross classes:

- **Storm-wyrms** (`Velkoraen` × `Aelvorath`) — winged dragons proper.
- **Balrogs proper** (`Drathalon` × `Vorthalen`) — fire-titans of the canonical Tolkien-adjacent type.
- **Vampire-king-class** (`Krevoraen` × `Skaelmorath`) — undead-deathless rulers with curse-blood feeding.
- **Sky-chimeras** (`Mirathven` × `Aelvorath`) — avian-feline-aspected hybrid-form.
- **Plague-swarms** (`Vesselrath` × `Phaelvenor`) — rot-bearing locust apocalypse, the canonical world-historical-cataclysm hybrid.
- **Wrong-deep-things** (`Drovenrath` × `Vothorisk`) — the kin most feared by mortal mage-orders.
- **Walking-mountain-class** (`Drathalon` × `Athravenel`) — the canonical "mountain that strides."
- **Blood-shifters** (`Krevoraen` × `Khaethorom`) — vampire-werewolf-doppelganger crossings.
- **Hellforge-knights** (`Skaelmorath` × `Vorthalen`) — fire-shrouded wraith-knights.
- **Gentled** (a special class within `Vesselrath`'s line, characterized by their use of contagion to *end* suffering rather than spread it; see [Vesselrath canon](vesselrath.md)).

Hybrid-cross classes do **not** count toward the Vorothar count of fourteen; they are derived lesser-kin lineages.

## Naming Conventions

Each beast-descendant class typically has:

- A **common name** in mortal-civilization usage (English-equivalent in this corpus: "dragon," "kraken," "vampire").
- A **primal-language headword** at lesser-beast scale, when canonized; some classes have their own root, others use compound-form from their parent Vorothar's root.
- Continent-local names that vary by mortal tradition.

The canonical primal-language headwording for individual derived classes is downstream; this page does not coin them.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Beast-descendant classes are derived, not direct Creator-creations.
- They inherit dilute mana-flavor from their progenitor(s).
- They populate the lesser-beast space of tier 5 and the upper edge of tier 6 monster slots.
- Dragons are descended primarily from `Velkoraen`; specific sub-lineages and hybrid-crosses are continent-local.
- Hybrid-cross classes between two Vorothar lineages are canonical and do not count toward the Vorothar fourteen.
- Beast-descendant classes can be killed by mortal might in extremis (unlike Vorothar themselves).

## Canon Boundaries

- Per-class full canon (named individual dragons, named manticores, named vampire-dynasties, etc.) is downstream and continent-local.
- The exact boundary between "lesser legendary beast" (tier 5) and "monster" (tier 6) is somewhat fluid and is set per-class. Generally: a derived class with cosmic-significance enough to have its own folk-memory and primal-language headword is tier-5; a derived class that mortals encounter as dangerous wildlife without cosmic-naming is tier-6.
- Whether any given hybrid-cross class can itself produce stable lineages of its own (cross-of-cross offspring) is open canon and likely continent-local.
- The primal-language naming of each derived class is open and is downstream coinage work.
