# Athravenel — The Wild-Heart

`Athravenel`, the **Wild-Heart**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose body is land that woke up wrong. The progenitor of every haunted forest, malicious mountain, plant-titan, treant-class, and place-as-monster whose shape is geographical rather than animal.

## Aspect and Domain

`Athravenel` is the cosmic principle of woken-place sovereignty. The descended kin are not creatures that occupy a place; they **are** the place, raised to a level mortals can no longer ignore. A haunted forest is `Athravenel`-kin: not a forest in which something dwells, but a forest that itself has intent. A malicious mountain is the same: not a mountain on which something resides, but a mountain whose own being has chosen against the mortals who cross it. The lineage is the cosmic answer to: what happens when geography wakes up?

## Visual Form

`Athravenel`'s body is a continent's worth of landscape, alive. Mortal accounts describe `Athravenel` as **standing across a region like a single great organism**: the forests are her hair, the rivers are her veins, the mountains are her vertebrae, the plains are her skin. There is no single body to point at; the whole region is the body. Some traditions render `Athravenel` with a humanoid avatar as well — a vegetal-titanic figure that walks within the larger territorial body — but the canonical truth is that the territory itself is the being.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Athravenel` mana-flavor is **rooted territory**. It feels like the awareness that one is standing somewhere, and that the somewhere is paying attention. Mortals who attune to `Athravenel` learn the discipline of *standing-in-relation-to-place*: their work draws power from the specific geography around them; their magic is location-bound; they cannot easily transport their craft to other lands. Druidic and geomantic traditions in mortal civilization trace founding-mana to `Athravenel`.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Wild-Heart's constellation sees `Athravenel` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern, but the perspective is unusual: the viewer sees a landscape rather than a creature. The landscape is alive — leaves rustle in coordinated patterns, water flows with intent, rocks shift with the slow patience of deliberation. `Athravenel` is **aware** through the entire territory at once. The reply, when it comes, is rarely in words; instead, the gazer's surrounding actual landscape (whatever continent they happen to be in) seems briefly to acknowledge them. Mortals who have received this reply often report a lifelong sensitivity to genius-loci of all places afterward.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Athravenel` is **aware** of every place-spirit kin, of every haunted-forest, of every malicious mountain, and of mortal travelers passing through territory she or her kin claim. Prayer to `Athravenel` is canonical for: safe passage through wild lands, the establishment of new settlements (where the petitioner acknowledges the prior territorial claim), the awakening of dormant local genius-loci to act in defense of their land. The Wild-Heart is the cosmic patron of all "do not anger the spirits of this place" wisdom.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Athravenel` dwells throughout an entire continent — that continent itself is her dormant body (specific continent open canon). The dormancy is partial: while the continental-scale body is asleep, smaller territorial sub-bodies (regional kin) are routinely awake and active. A traveler crossing the Wild-Heart's continent is, in folk-memory, never quite alone; the territory itself attends.

## Stirring

`Athravenel` stirs:
- when mortal civilization commits acts of large-scale geographic violence on the dwelling-continent (clearcutting, mountain-leveling, river-rerouting at industrial scale)
- when the descended kin are extirpated en masse from their local territories
- when a foreign tier-3 or tier-4 force attempts to claim sovereignty over the dwelling-continent's territory
- when the multi-millennial mythic cycle renews

A stirred `Athravenel` is continental: the entire dwelling-continent becomes hostile to mortal civilization. Forests grow in unfortunate directions, mountains shift to block roads, rivers flood crops, weather turns punitive, the wildlife organizes against settlements. Defeat is by ritual-restitution rather than combat: mortal civilization makes formal restitution, abandons certain territories, acknowledges the prior claim, and `Athravenel` returns to dormancy. There is no mortal force that can defeat her by violence; the continent itself fights back.

## Descended Kin

The wild-heart kin lineage includes: haunted forests (regional, often with named central trees or groves), malicious mountains (regional sovereign peaks that turn against trespassers), plant-titans (mobile vegetal-cataclysm-class beings, distinct from `Drathalon`'s humanoid titans), treant-class (tree-form humanoid-near beings, often allied with mortal druids), woken-cave-systems (subterranean place-spirits), poisoned-marsh genius (often allied with `Vesselrath`'s line), and the *traveling* class (rare; place-spirits who have, by unknown means, become mobile and now wander as autonomous landscape-beings).

Hybrid offspring: `Athravenel` × `Drathalon` produces walking-mountain-class kin (the canonical "mountain that strides"); `Athravenel` × `Vesselrath` produces poisoned-territory kin; `Athravenel` × `Vothorisk` produces wrong-territory kin (regions whose geometry does not stably match mortal cartography).

## Mythic Role

`Athravenel` in folk-memory is the cosmic patron of the genius-loci tradition. Druidic, shamanic, and geomantic mortal traditions trace founding-rites to encounters with `Athravenel`'s line. She is the cosmic ground of all "the land remembers" wisdom across mortal cultures. Settlements built without acknowledging her or her local kin are folk-memory-canonically prone to long-term failure; settlements built in proper relation are folk-memory-canonically blessed across generations.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Athravenel`'s dwelling continent is open canon.
- The exact form of any humanoid avatar (whether `Athravenel` projects one) is open and may be set by local mythic tradition.
- Specific named place-spirits (regional haunted forests, named malicious mountains, treant-class elders) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Athravenel`'s mana and mortal druidic/geomantic traditions is rich and is downstream lore.
- The interaction between `Athravenel`'s territorial sovereignty and the political sovereignty of mortal kingdoms is continent-local mythic tradition.
