# Krevoraen — The Bloodborn-Sire

`Krevoraen`, the **Bloodborn-Sire**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose curse takes humanoid form and turns it predator. The progenitor of every vampire, werewolf, ghoul, wendigo, curse-bound humanoid champion, and blood-cursed lineage that retains a humanoid silhouette while losing the rest of mortal kinship.

**Important:** `Krevoraen` is **categorically distinct from the humanic mortal race**, which is a tier-6 light-affinity humanoid race derived from a Creator-created Adam/Eve. The shared humanoid body-shape is a folk-conflation; the canonical lineages are wholly separate. See "Boundaries" below.

## Aspect and Domain

`Krevoraen` is the cosmic principle of cursed humanoid predation. The descended kin **look like mortals at first glance**, and that resemblance is the danger. They live among mortal civilization, often unrecognized; they prey upon mortals across generations; they reproduce by spreading the curse rather than (only) by birth. The lineage is the cosmic answer to: what does a beast become when its hunting-ground is mortal civilization, and its disguise is mortal form?

## Visual Form

`Krevoraen`'s body is described in folk-memory as **a humanoid figure of impossible age and constant predation**. Tall, thin, pale or red-skinned (varies by tradition), with elongated canines visible behind a closed mouth, eyes that catch light like an animal's, hands that end in faintly curved nails. The body is recognizably humanoid; the *kind* of humanoid is the wrongness — too still, too watchful, too patient. Folk-memory holds that an unwary observer might mistake `Krevoraen` for an ordinary mortal in poor health, and that this is the moment the Bloodborn-Sire prefers.

This figure is **not** the humanic mortal Adam (or Eve). The humanic mortal firsts are full mortals at the unreachable Eden; their bodies are not predator-bodies; they are not progenitors of vampires or werewolves. The two should not be conflated.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Krevoraen` mana-flavor is **the predator at the table**. It feels like the realization that someone present has been measuring you for some time. Mortals who attune to `Krevoraen` (rare; the discipline involves some encounter with the curse-line) develop heightened predatory instincts: they read crowds for vulnerability, they navigate social settings as if they were hunting-grounds, they value patience over speed. Hunter-mage-orders of mortal civilization use this mana inverted — they learn to *recognize* the predator-at-the-table, to feel `Krevoraen`-mana when it walks past. The hunters and the hunted draw from adjacent wells.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Bloodborn-Sire's constellation sees `Krevoraen` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern, the humanoid figure clear and watching. `Krevoraen` is **aware** and may **reply**. The reply is canonical for its courtesy: the Sire is, in folk-memory, *polite* — speaks softly, does not waste mortal time, addresses the gazer by their proper name and station. The courtesy is the danger. Mortals who have received `Krevoraen`'s courtesy describe a subsequent inability to feel safe in mortal company for some time afterward; the courtesy registered as the manner of a predator that found them edible.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Krevoraen` is **aware** of every blood-cursed kin descendant and of every mortal currently afflicted by the curse-lines. The Sire takes a **personal interest** in major curse-events: a mortal champion newly turned, a vampire-line establishing a new dynastic foothold, a werewolf-pack reaching cosmic-significant numbers. Prayer to `Krevoraen` is uncommon and dangerous; in folk-memory, the prayed-upon mortal often becomes a candidate for the curse themselves. Hunter-mage-orders explicitly forbid prayer to the Sire; demonologist-style negotiations are reserved for the most extreme circumstances.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Krevoraen` dwells dormant in a ruined mortal-style castle on a major island of [Seralinor](../places/seralinor.md), the Sea-Threading archipelago. The site is folk-known but rarely visited; mortals who approach report a sense of being watched from windows that should be empty, of the architecture being *maintained* in a way that suggests presence rather than abandonment, of the surrounding region being unusually free of ordinary wildlife. The dormancy is shallow; folk-memory holds that `Krevoraen` is closer to wakefulness than most `Vorothar`, because his work is conducted continuously through his descended kin across the islands of Seralinor and beyond. The hunter-mage-orders headquartered on Seralinor maintain canonical wards against the dwelling-island.

## Stirring

`Krevoraen` stirs:
- when a major curse-line is broken by mortal hunter-orders at a continental scale
- when descended blood-cursed kin face mass-extinction (rare; the stirring is restorative, with new kin created)
- when a mortal civilization develops technological or magical means to detect the curse-line at scale (this directly threatens the lineage's survival mode)
- when the multi-millennial mythic cycle renews

A stirred `Krevoraen` is socially apocalyptic: vampire-dynasties activate, werewolf-packs coordinate at continental scale, ghoul-cataclysms emerge in cities, hunter-orders are decimated. The defeat is canonically uncertain — the Sire is too socially-embedded, too distributed, too patient. Folk-memory records "rebound to castle," not final defeat.

## Descended Kin

The blood-cursed kin lineage includes: vampires (long-lived, blood-feeding, often dynastic; the most numerous lesser-kin lineage), werewolves (cycle-curse, lunar-aspected, often regional rural in mortal contact), ghouls (corpse-eating, often urban-underground, sometimes confused with `Vesselrath`'s rot-bearers), wendigos (cannibal-curse, often regional cold-continent traditions), curse-bound humanoid champions (single-mortal-curse cases who became cataclysmic in their own right), and the *settled* class (curse-bearers who have, through mortal-rite or mage-craft, stabilized their predation into sustainable patterns — sometimes allied with mortal civilization in carefully-bounded ways).

Hybrid offspring: `Krevoraen` × `Skaelmorath` produces blood-wraiths (the canonical "vampire-king of folk-memory" — undead-deathless rulers with curse-blood feeding); `Krevoraen` × `Vorthalen` produces blood-fiends (vampire-demon hybrids); `Krevoraen` × `Khaethorom` produces blood-shifters (vampire-werewolf-doppelganger crossings, the most dangerous of all to mortal authentication-orders).

## Mythic Role

`Krevoraen` in folk-memory is the cosmic ground of every "monster wearing a familiar face" tradition. Hunter-orders, blood-mage academies, vampire-bane traditions, and werewolf-curse rites all trace founding-myths to encounters with `Krevoraen`'s line. The Bloodborn-Sire is also the canonical example of the predator-that-cannot-be-defeated-once-and-for-all: every generation must fight him again, because his kin are reproducing in mortal communities even now.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Krevoraen`'s dwelling castle/estate's continental location is open canon.
- The categorical distinction between `Krevoraen`'s tier-5 cursed-humanoid kin and the tier-6 humanic mortal race is **hard canon**: the two lineages are wholly separate, share no progenitor, and are not interchangeable in any context.
- Specific named curse-line kin (named vampire dynasties, regional werewolf-pack traditions, named wendigo-cataclysms) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Krevoraen`'s curse-mana and the [Vatharen](../astrology/celestials.md) (especially `Yirmor` Decay and `Aenor` Severance) is open research-frontier; canonically the lines are distinct (force vs beast), but practitioners report convergent effects.
- The full theology of curse-pact magic, blood-line transmission rules, and the difference between "born cursed" vs "turned cursed" is rich downstream territory.
- Whether `Krevoraen` was always cursed or became cursed (vs being "the source of the curse" by Creator-design) is canonically unsettled and may vary by mortal tradition.
