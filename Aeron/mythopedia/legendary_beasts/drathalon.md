# Drathalon — The Colossus-Father

`Drathalon`, the **Colossus-Father**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) who walks at mountain-scale. The titan-of-titans, whose footfall reshapes coastlines, whose stride spans valleys, whose silhouette can be mistaken on the horizon for a peak that has begun to move.

## Aspect and Domain

`Drathalon` is the cosmic principle of humanoid mass-scale catastrophe. The body is recognizably humanoid — two arms, two legs, a head bearing eyes and a mouth — but the scale is past plausibility, and the consequence of presence is geological. `Drathalon`'s descended kin are titans, jotnar, ettins, balrogs, oni, formorians: beings whose general shape mortals can grasp because it resembles their own, but whose mass makes that resemblance the most disturbing detail.

## Visual Form

`Drathalon` is humanoid in silhouette and mountain-scale in mass. The body is muscled in proportion to its scale — if the body were mortal-sized, it would still be massive; at its actual scale, the muscling is the size of forests. The face is mortal-comprehensible: brow, eyes, nose, mouth in the canonical positions, with the relative proportions of an adult mortal magnified. Hair, beard, and bodily covering vary by tradition; some folk-memory shows `Drathalon` armored, others bare-skinned, others clothed in actual continent-textiles (a forest as a beard, a river as a sash). Eye color is recorded as iron-grey across most mortal traditions.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Drathalon` mana-flavor is **foundational mass**. It feels like the unanswerable weight of a mountain placed where a mountain has every right to be. Mortals who attune to `Drathalon` learn the discipline of being unmovable — not stubbornness, but gravitas. Battle-mages of titan-kin lineages bear arms and armor that, by mana-resonance, refuse to be lifted by anyone unfaithful to the lineage; civic leaders who pray to `Drathalon` invoke a presence that can quiet a riot by simple mass.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Colossus-Father's constellation sees `Drathalon` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern. The viewer perceives him from below — even at peak attunement, the view is upward. The face is calm; the breathing is slow on a scale mortals find difficult to track; the eye-blink is measured in long mortal seconds. `Drathalon` is **aware** and may **reply** with a single word, or sometimes with a single physical gesture. The gesture, if seen, is canonically a nod — neither approving nor disapproving, but acknowledging. Mortals who receive the nod often spend the rest of their lives interpreting it.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Drathalon` is **aware** of all titan-kin descendants on Aeron and feels their dishonor or honor as personal weight. Mortal prayer to `Drathalon` is canonically rare and weighty; the Colossus-Father does not respond to flippant petition. Petitions of war, oath, mass, sovereignty, and last-stand defense are the canonical forms. Mortal warriors before great battles invoke `Drathalon` for the steadiness rather than the strike.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Drathalon` dwells dormant either in a seated posture along a mountain range that bears his outline (legend says one continent has a "sleeping titan range") or in a prone posture beneath a great plain that subtly rises and falls with breathing on a multi-millennial cycle. Either way, mortal civilization that lives over the dwelling has long-standing taboos against deep mining or great construction at certain folk-known sites. The taboos are usually, in folk-memory, justified by stories of failed kingdoms.

## Stirring

`Drathalon` stirs:
- when a titan-kin descendant is killed dishonorably (by deception or by overwhelming numbers in a way that implies mortal cowardice)
- when a great oath is broken at continental scale
- when a mortal civilization breaks the dwelling-taboo with mining, construction, or war
- when the multi-millennial cycle of his own mythic time renews

A stirred `Drathalon` is geologically catastrophic on a scale that takes weeks to measure: the stride displaces lakes, the breath shapes weather across hemispheres, the voice damages mortal hearing across continents. Defeat of a stirred `Drathalon` is canonically held to require the cooperation of a pantheon-leader god, multiple lesser beasts of opposed lineages, and a fated-being hero whose Class 2 star may ascend as a consequence.

## Descended Kin

The titan-kin lineage of `Drathalon` is wide. Standard sub-lineages include: titans (closest to the sire's form, century-scale lifespans), jotnar (frost-aspected, north-continent-dwelling), ettins (multi-headed, often two-headed, civic-aspected), balrogs (fire-aspected, often misidentified by mortal scholars as `Vorthalen`'s kin), oni (regional eastern-continent variants, masked face-aspected), formorians (oceanic-bordered, malformed-aspected, often mistaken for `Drovenrath`'s kin).

Hybrid offspring: `Drathalon` × `Athravenel` produces walking-mountain-class kin (rare and continent-defining when they appear); `Drathalon` × `Vorthalen` produces fire-titan-class kin (the proper canonical balrogs, which are dual-line); `Drathalon` × `Krevoraen` produces blood-titan curse-warriors (very rare).

## Mythic Role

`Drathalon` is, in Aeron's folk-memory, the ground-state of "great mortal hero made cosmic." Heroes who aspire to titan-stature do not ascend to `Drathalon`'s line (heroes ascend to tier 4, not tier 5), but they invoke `Drathalon` as the cosmic precedent for their aspiration. Cultures with titan-cult traditions hold annual festivals at the dwelling-site or its mortal-known proxies, and read `Drathalon`'s constellation for civic-time omens.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Drathalon`'s dwelling continent is open canon.
- The exact form of the constellation `Drathalon` occupies is open; folk-memory most often holds it as a humanoid silhouette but the specific star-arrangement is downstream.
- Specific named titan-kin (individual jotnar, named oni-lords) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Drathalon` and any local sovereignty (whether titan-kin worship of mortal kings, mortal-king worship of titan-kin, or mutual indifference) is continent-local mythic tradition.
