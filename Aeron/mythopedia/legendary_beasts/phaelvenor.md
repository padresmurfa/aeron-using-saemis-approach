# Phaelvenor — The Swarm-Voice

`Phaelvenor`, the **Swarm-Voice**, is the [Vorothar](../../primal_language/structural_principles/vorothar.md) whose body is many bodies and whose voice is many voices. The progenitor of every locust-god, gestalt-collective, devouring-swarm, and many-mouthed cataclysm.

## Aspect and Domain

`Phaelvenor` is the cosmic principle of unified multitude. The descended kin are not collections of individuals; they are single beings whose body happens to be distributed across thousands or millions of subordinate forms. A locust-god is not a swarm of locusts; it is one being shaped like a swarm of locusts. Killing one locust does not diminish the being; the being merely reshapes around the loss.

## Visual Form

`Phaelvenor` is described in folk-memory as a **shifting cloud of small bodies**, each body individually animal-form (insectile in most traditions, avian in some, rodent in regional variants), but the cloud-shape persists with continuity even as individual bodies die and others are born within it. The cloud has a vague humanoid silhouette in some traditions, a vague vortex in others, a vague mountain in still others. The voice is canonical: a single voice composed of many small voices speaking simultaneously and in unison, mortal-comprehensible only because the small voices align perfectly.

## Mana-Flavor

The `Phaelvenor` mana-flavor is **aggregate authority**. It feels like the weight of many small wills aligned into a single decision. Mortals who attune to `Phaelvenor` learn the discipline of collective work: their commands move groups; their oaths are sworn by many voices at once; their armies march in unnaturally coordinated rhythm. Mages of `Phaelvenor`'s mana lineage are rarely solitary practitioners; the work is itself communal.

## Gaze-Resolution at Peak Attunement

A fully attuned viewer of the Swarm-Voice's constellation sees `Phaelvenor` positioned between themselves and the sky-pattern, but the position is multiple — the viewer perceives the beast at many points around themselves simultaneously. `Phaelvenor` is **aware** through every body in the swarm at once. The reply, when it comes, is in the unison-voice, and is canonically louder and clearer than any other `Vorothar`'s reply because the swarm-voice can address mortal hearing in a way that more focused beings cannot.

## Awareness and Interaction

`Phaelvenor` is **aware** of every swarm-kin descendant continuously and **aware** of every mortal whose collective decision has reached `Phaelvenor`-resonance (mass-vote, mass-prayer, mass-uprising). Prayer to `Phaelvenor` works best when delivered by groups; solitary prayer often goes unanswered, while the same prayer chanted by many receives focused reply. `Phaelvenor` is the canonical patron of mass-movements, mob-mages, and any mortal craft that depends on collective force.

## Dwelling and Dormancy

`Phaelvenor` does not dwell at a single point. The dwelling-substrate is a continent (specific continent open canon), and within that continent the swarm-body is dispersed across thousands of square miles, with the small bodies individually replaceable and the swarm-as-a-whole reconvening at folk-known sites for canonical assembly. Mortal travelers in the dwelling-continent learn to recognize the signs: birds gathering against the wind, insects accumulating in unusual densities, small mammals moving in synchronized lines.

## Stirring

`Phaelvenor` stirs:
- when mass-mortal-events occur at sufficient intensity (rebellion, mass migration, plague-evacuation)
- when descended swarm-kin numbers approach cosmic threshold (whether by die-off or by overgrowth)
- when a mortal mage attempts to fragment the swarm by selectively killing the most influential subordinate bodies (this rarely works, and the stirring is wrathful)

A stirred `Phaelvenor` is the locust-cloud-as-cataclysm: a continent darkened for a season, agriculture destroyed across kingdoms, mortal civilization forced into rationed survival. The defeat is not by killing but by ritual-coordination across pantheons: many gods pray-with mortals, pantheon-leaders cooperate, the swarm-voice is given a counter-voice and chooses to disperse.

## Descended Kin

The swarm-kin lineage includes: locust-gods (insect-form, agricultural-cataclysm), devouring-swarms (mixed-species, predatory), gestalt-collectives (sentient mass-mind beings whose individual bodies are humanoid-near forms), many-mouthed beasts (single-body multi-mouth predators that read as swarm by virtue of their multiplicity-of-attack), hive-cataclysms (geographically-anchored swarm-cities).

Hybrid offspring: `Phaelvenor` × `Vesselrath` produces plague-swarms (the canonical apocalyptic combination — rot-bearing locusts); `Phaelvenor` × `Aelvorath` produces sky-swarms (storm-aligned bird-cataclysm); `Phaelvenor` × `Khoramor` produces consuming-swarms.

## Mythic Role

`Phaelvenor` in folk-memory is the cosmic principle that mortal civilization invokes when speaking of "the people speaking with one voice." Some mortal traditions treat this as benediction; others as warning. Cult-traditions of the Swarm-Voice are rarely mass-membership (mortals fear their own collective being more than they fear most beasts) but are often institutionally embedded in mass-decision-making.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Phaelvenor`'s dwelling continent is open canon.
- The exact dominant-species of the swarm-body (insect, avian, rodent, etc.) is open and may be set by the dwelling continent's local mythic tradition.
- Specific named swarm-kin (regional locust-gods, named hive-cataclysm cities) are downstream lesser-beast canon.
- The relationship between `Phaelvenor`'s mana and mortal democratic or collective political traditions is left open as a thematic resonance rather than a canonical claim.
