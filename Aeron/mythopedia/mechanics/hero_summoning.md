# Hero-Summoning, Reincarnation, and Prophecy

This page canonizes the cosmic mechanism by which fated beings — heroes, dragons, kings, saints, demon-lords, individuals favored by gods or beasts — can be **identified** through their Class 2 fated-being star, **summoned** through ritual targeting, **reincarnated** across worlds, and **prophesied** before they are born. All three (summoning, reincarnation, prophecy) rest on the same substrate: the [Star-Fate Doctrine (R2 + positional-weighting)](../astrology/star_fate.md).

## Doctrine

A **fated being** has a [Class 2 fated-being star](../astrology/star_fate.md) born with them. The star tracks the being's life-arc and is positioned at the weighted centroid of the [Class 1 lexical-witness stars](../astrology/star_fate.md) for the primal-language words in the being's fate.

This means: **the star encodes the being's fate.** A scholar who can read the primal-word neighborhood of a Class 2 star can recover the major fate-words, and from those, infer enough about the being to perform rituals targeting them — across distance, across death, across worlds.

The mechanism enables three related cosmic operations:

1. **Hero-summoning** — calling the fated being into the present moment.
2. **Reincarnation** — the same fated being's star may reignite in a new life, and rituals can recognize the continuity.
3. **Prophecy** — predicting the rise of a fated being from the patterning of primal-word stars before the being is born.

## The Mechanism

### Identifying the Star

A scholar identifies a Class 2 fated-being star by:

1. **Distinguishing it from Class 1.** Class 2 stars exhibit position-drift, brightness-fluctuation, and historical correlation with mortal-historical events.
2. **Mapping its primal-word neighborhood.** The star's position falls within or near a cluster of Class 1 stars; those Class 1 stars correspond to primal-language words.
3. **Inferring fate-words and weights.** From the cluster's geometry around the Class 2 star, the scholar infers which primal words are most central to the fated being's fate (highest-weight) and which are peripheral.
4. **Recovering enough of the fate to act.** With major fate-words identified, the scholar has enough cosmic-handle to ritually target the being.

This process requires deep training. Mortal mage-orders dedicated to it (often called *star-readers* or *fate-cartographers* in folk-memory) maintain canonical Class-1 catalogs and tradition-specific reading techniques.

### Summoning Ritual

Once the fated being is identified by their fate-words, a **summoning ritual** can call them into the present:

- The ritual invokes each fate-word in sequence, weighted by the inferred importance.
- The ritual provides a **vector** (spatial or symbolic location for the summoned being to arrive at) and a **purpose** (what the summoner wants from the being).
- The fated being's mana-channel responds — they appear, are drawn into a dream, are turned toward the summoner across a distance, or in extreme cases are physically translocated.

Summoning requires the fated being's **cooperation at the cosmic-mana level**. A being who actively resists the summoning can refuse — though the resistance has cost. A being who cooperates is brought to the ritual's vector.

**Cross-world summoning** is canonical. A fated being from a world other than Aeron — perhaps from a parallel cosmic frame, or from a separate created cosmos — can be summoned to Aeron via the same mechanism, provided their Class 2 star is identifiable from Aeron's sky. This is the canonical Aeron mechanism for the "isekai-class" hero-summoning trope.

### Reincarnation

A fated being's death does not extinguish their fate. The Class 2 star persists for as long as the being's legend persists — sometimes for centuries after death.

If the being is **reincarnated** (canonically, they return to mortal life as a new mortal carrying the same fate), the Class 2 star **flares anew**:

- Brightness flares first.
- Position shifts to reflect the new life's emerging fate-words.
- Mortal historical record correlates with the flare.

A reincarnated being is therefore identifiable: their old star reignites with new emphasis. Star-readers can confirm reincarnation events by tracking the same Class 2 star across multiple flare-cycles.

The mechanism for reincarnation itself is open canon and varies by mortal tradition; some hold the soul is reborn directly, others that the fate-pattern recruits a new mortal, others that pantheon-gods or beasts arrange the rebirth deliberately. The **canonical fact** is that the star persists, and rituals that target the star can target the reincarnated being.

### Prophecy

The reverse direction also works: **before** a fated being is born, the primal-word stars of their incipient fate begin to *cluster* in characteristic patterns. A skilled astrologer reading the sky for impending fate-events can detect:

- **Cluster-tightening** — Class 1 stars in particular primal-word groups become readable as a coherent pattern despite being scattered (an anticipatory cluster-effect; the cosmic structure is preparing).
- **Pre-birth flare** — in some traditions, a Class 2 star may briefly flare and then dim before the fated being is actually born, giving a multi-year prophecy window.
- **Sympathetic resonance** — nearby Class 2 stars of related fates may shift slightly to acknowledge the impending arrival.

Mortal prophecy of the form "a hero will rise" or "a demon-lord will be born" is canonically a star-reader's interpretation of these patterns. The prophecy is **not** a Creator-revealed message; it is a mortal mage's reading of what the cosmic substrate is already arranging.

### Demon-Lord Summoning and Adversarial Cases

The summoning mechanism is **morally neutral**. A fated being whose fate is destructive — a demon-lord, a tyrant, a great wraith — can be summoned by the same mechanism that summons benevolent heroes. The summoner's intent is independent of the cosmic mechanism.

This is why hero-summoning rituals are canonically **expensive and dangerous**. A poorly-prepared or insufficiently-careful summoner may:

- Summon a being whose fate proves adversarial to the summoner's intent.
- Misidentify a Class 2 star and summon the wrong being.
- Trigger sympathetic reactions in nearby Class 2 stars (other fated beings drawn into the ritual unintentionally).
- Activate hostile awareness from the fated being themselves.

Mage-orders that practice summoning typically maintain extensive sympathetic-magic safeguards.

## Cost of Summoning

Cosmic operations of this scale carry cost. Canonical cost-categories:

- **Mana cost.** The ritual draws on the summoner's (and supporting circle's) mana reserves at scales sometimes exceeding mortal individual capacity. Summoners often work in concert with multiple supporting mages.
- **Star-disturbance cost.** The Class 2 star is briefly disturbed; its drift-pattern may be permanently altered by the summoning. This is detectable by other star-readers and is itself a public event in the astrological community.
- **Fated-being awareness cost.** The being becomes aware of being summoned. Subsequent dealings with them are affected.
- **Cosmic-attention cost.** Tier-3 Vatharen, tier-4 gods, or tier-5 beasts whose own fate is entangled with the summoned being may take notice. Adverse attention from these higher tiers is canonically a real risk.

The cost scales with: distance (cross-world summoning is most expensive), the resistance of the summoned being, and the ambition of the summoning purpose.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Hero-summoning, reincarnation, and prophecy all rest on the [Star-Fate Doctrine (R2 + positional-weighting)](../astrology/star_fate.md).
- A Class 2 star encodes the fated being's fate via the primal-word neighborhood pattern.
- Summoning requires identification of fate-words, ritual invocation, and the fated being's cosmic-mana cooperation (which may be refused at cost).
- Cross-world summoning is canonical.
- Reincarnation flares the same star anew with shifted fate-words.
- Prophecy is the star-reader's interpretation of incipient cluster-patterns; it is not Creator-revelation.
- The mechanism is morally neutral. It works for adversarial summons (demon-lord, tyrant) as well as benevolent ones.
- Summoning carries real cost: mana, star-disturbance, awareness, cosmic-attention.

## Canon Boundaries

- Specific named summoning rituals, mage-order traditions, and historical summoning events are downstream and continent-local.
- The metaphysics of *what* moves between worlds in cross-world summoning (does a body translocate? a soul? a fate-pattern that rebuilds a body on arrival?) is open canon and varies by ritual.
- The relationship between hero-summoning at tier-6 and the elevation of heroes to tier-4 demi-godhood is open: typically a summoned hero is still a tier-6 mortal, but elevation can occur during or after the summoning event, particularly for cross-world summons whose presence on Aeron itself constitutes a fate-shifting event.
- Whether summoning rituals can be defeated by counter-magic that breaks the Class 2 star itself is open and is a research-frontier in adversarial mage-craft.
- The canonical mortal cost of repeated summoning by the same mage-order over generations is open and may produce its own historical patterns (long-running summoner-orders eventually exhausting their mana-channel access, mage-orders that summon too often becoming unable to summon at all, etc.).
