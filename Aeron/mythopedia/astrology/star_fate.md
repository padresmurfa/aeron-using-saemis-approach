# Star-Fate Doctrine

This page canonizes the **R2 two-class stars doctrine** with the **positional-weighting mechanic** for fated-being stars. The doctrine resolves the relation between the existing lexical-witness star canon and the new fated-being star design. Both classes coexist in Aeron's sky.

## Doctrine

The sky of Aeron carries two coexisting classes of [`Taviloren` (stars)](../../primal_language/structural_principles/taviloren.md):

### Class 1 — Lexical-Witness Stars (Eternal)

Every primal-language word has a permanent corresponding `Taviloren` bearing the same name. The sky is a visible lexicon of creation. The star does not create the word, and the word does not create the star; both witness the same underlying truth.

**Class 1 stars are fixed across creation.** They do not drift. They have been present since the [Daravelon (Reionization Era)](../cosmology/reionization_era.md) ignition of stable created lights, and their positions are the substrate of Aeron's sky-lexicon. They are the reference frame against which all other sky phenomena are read.

Class 1 stars vary in brightness — foundational primal-language words have brighter stars; specialized or structurally narrow words have dimmer stars. But their **positions** are fixed.

### Class 2 — Fated-Being Stars (Transient)

A **fated being** — a dragon, a king, a hero, a saint, an individual favored by gods — has a star born with them. The star tracks the trajectory of their life-arc:

- **Born** with the fated being.
- **Brightens** in proportion to the being's deeds and legend during their living years.
- **Mirrors** the being's life — moves slowly during slow seasons; flares during decisive moments; dims during exile, illness, or doubt.
- **Persists** after the being's death for as long as their legend persists.
- **Fades** as the legend fades. When the last living mortal who remembers the being forgets them, the Class 2 star fades to invisibility (though faint trace residues may persist).

Class 2 stars are the second sky-layer riding on top of the eternal Class 1 substrate.

## Positional-Weighting Mechanic

A Class 2 star's **position in the sky is the weighted centroid of the Class-1 lexical-witness stars corresponding to the primal-language words associated with the fated being's fate**.

The fate is held as an **ordered weighted list of primal words**. Each word has a weight reflecting how central it is to the being's destiny. The Class 2 star occupies the position calculated as:

> position = weighted average of (Class 1 stars for fate-words, weighted by importance to the fate)

As the fated being's fate evolves — as they mature, deviate from destiny, deepen an attunement, or crystallize a new aspect — the **weights shift, and the star drifts**. A sudden shift in a Class 2 star's position is an astrological event; trained astrologers read it as the fated being undergoing a fate-altering moment.

### Reading the Star

Scholars who can identify a Class 2 star can:

1. **Locate it** against the fixed Class 1 backdrop.
2. **Identify the nearest Class 1 stars** by their primal-language correspondences.
3. **Infer the fated being's fate-words** from the cluster of nearby Class 1 stars.
4. **Estimate weights** from how close the Class 2 star sits to each Class 1 neighbor.

This is the foundational mechanism for [hero-summoning, reincarnation, and prophecy](../mechanics/hero_summoning.md). A scholar who reads a Class 2 star with sufficient skill knows enough about the fated being's fate to perform rituals targeting them.

### The Drift

Class 2 star drift is normally slow — comparable to mortal generation timescales for ordinary fate-evolutions. Sudden drift is rare and consequential:

- **Reincarnation flare:** A reincarnated fated being's star flares anew, and may shift position abruptly to reflect the new life's fate-words.
- **Threshold-deed drift:** When a fated being commits an act that decisively shifts their fate (a coronation, a great betrayal, a covenant-of-blood), their star drifts perceptibly within hours or days.
- **Death-shift:** A fated being's death may cause the star to shift toward their post-mortem-legend fate-words (e.g., from "warrior" toward "martyred liberator").
- **Elevation-shift:** When a Class 2 star is about to ascend into a galaxy as a tier-4 god-cluster, it drifts toward the galaxy and stabilizes around the words that will be the god's domain.

## Class-Distinguishing in Astrology

A trained astrologer must distinguish Class 1 from Class 2. Standard signs of a Class 2 star:

- **Brightness fluctuation** on mortal-life timescales (Class 1 brightness is steady).
- **Position drift** against Class 1 background (Class 1 positions are fixed).
- **Birth and fade events** in mortal historical record (Class 1 stars have always been there).
- **Astrological correlation** with mortal-historical events (a star that brightens at the same time as a famous battle, then dims slowly over centuries).

Untrained mortals cannot easily distinguish the two. Mortal mage-orders maintain canonical Class-1 catalogs to enable trained astrologers to do the comparison.

## Hero-Summoning, Reincarnation, and Prophecy

The full mechanism of identifying a Class 2 star, reading its primal-word adjacency, and performing rituals targeting the fated being is canonized at [hero-summoning, reincarnation, and prophecy](../mechanics/hero_summoning.md). The R2 + positional-weighting doctrine is the necessary substrate of those mechanics.

Key implications:

- A hero whose star has not yet brightened to recognizable Class-2 visibility cannot be summoned by ritual; they are still too obscure.
- A fated being's death does not extinguish the ritual-target; the star persists as long as legend persists.
- Reincarnation creates new ritual-targets for the same underlying being.
- Prophecy of *who* will be born is read from primal-word patterns that have not yet seen a Class 2 star — the star is foreshadowed by the cluster's tendency before the being is born.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- Two classes of stars coexist: Class 1 (eternal lexical-witness, one per primal-language word, positions fixed) and Class 2 (transient fated-being, born with the being, positions drifting).
- Class 1 positions are fixed across creation. They do not drift.
- Class 2 star positions are the **weighted centroid of the Class-1 stars for the primal-words in the being's fate**, with the weights reflecting the importance of each fate-word to the being.
- As fate evolves, Class 2 stars drift against the Class 1 backdrop.
- The two classes coexist in the same sky; astrologers learn to distinguish them.
- Hero-summoning, reincarnation rituals, and prophecy all rest on this substrate.

## Canon Boundaries

- The exact algorithm by which fate-word weights are assigned to a fated being is canonically held to be a matter of cosmic fact rather than mortal calculation; mortal astrologers infer the weights, but do not compute them.
- The threshold of legend-strength at which a Class 2 star becomes visible is qualitative and continent-local in interpretation.
- The minimum number of fate-words a being must have to qualify for a Class 2 star is open canon (folk-memory implies "at least three significant words"; specialized scholarly traditions may differ).
- Whether a Class 2 star can be created by deliberate mortal mage-action (by manufacturing a fate-word collection in a mortal life) is open canon and is a research-frontier.
- The relationship between Class 2 star drift and the [Vatharen](celestials.md) (especially `Vaelvor` Time and `Vaelmor` Memory) is rich downstream territory.
