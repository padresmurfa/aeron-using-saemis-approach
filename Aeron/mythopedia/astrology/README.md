# Astrology

`astrology` records Aeron's canonical sky lore: luminaries, stars, constellations, moons, planet-like celestials, calendrical celestial rhythm, and omen-bearing sky events.

## Use
- Record celestial facts and astrology-facing interpretations that are canon, not mere folklore.
- Record the correspondence between primal-language words and their stars, including the research practice of astrologers.
- Keep creation-era cosmology aligned with planet-era observation and divination.
- Separate settled sky law from still-open astrological questions.

## Mythic-Hierarchy Sky Layers

Aeron's sky is layered. Each layer corresponds to a tier of the [mythic hierarchy](../cosmology/mythic_hierarchy.md):

- **Luminaries (Taviran)** — `Solaryth` and `Nyxorys`, the light/darklight axis: see `luminaries.md`.
- **Moons (Seravel)** — the seven moons of Aeron at tier 2: see `moons_of_aeron.md`.
- **Planet-like Celestials (Vatharen)** — the eight wandering force-bearers at tier 3: see `celestials.md`.
- **Galaxies (Pantheons)** — the five canonical pantheons at tier 4: see [`../cosmology/pantheons.md`](../cosmology/pantheons.md).
- **Constellations (Tavirath)** — the legendary beasts at tier 5: see `constellations.md` and [`../legendary_beasts/`](../legendary_beasts/).
- **Stars (Taviloren)** — Class 1 lexical-witness (eternal) and Class 2 fated-being (transient): see `stars.md`, `primal_stars.md`, and `star_fate.md`.

## Canon Boundaries

- `Daravelon` (Reionization Era) introduces the first created stars; `Kethavelen` (Stelliferous Era) stabilizes enduring star-realms and memory constellations; `Kethralen` (Planetary Formation Era) localizes celestial order into world-centered cycles.
- Astrologers study stars as a real research surface into primal-language meaning; they are not merely fortune-tellers.
- Class 1 and Class 2 stars coexist in the sky; astrologers learn to distinguish them; Class 1 positions are fixed, Class 2 positions drift with their fated being's life-arc.
- Exact numeric moon masses, orbital distances, eccentricities, and densities remain pending unless they are explicitly canonized elsewhere.
- Eclipse doctrine, asteroid canon, and the named celestial-sphere placement of the five galaxies and fourteen prominent Vorothar-constellations are open canon, gated to per-continent and per-pantheon downstream work.
