# The Firsts (Adam and Eve, Humanic and Demonic)

The **Firsts** are the four directly Creator-created mortal-tier beings: **humanic Adam, humanic Eve, demonic Adam, demonic Eve**. They are the cosmic origin-anchors of all mortal mana on Aeron. They live in [Eden](../places/eden.md), a real place on Aeron unreachable to mortals. They have not fallen.

## Identity

The Firsts are four:

| First | Race | Affinity | Alignment Bias | Pair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Humanic Adam | Humanic | Light (`Solaryth`-aspected) | Chaotic-biased gradient | Humanic Eve |
| Humanic Eve | Humanic | Light (`Solaryth`-aspected) | Chaotic-biased gradient | Humanic Adam |
| Demonic Adam | Demonic | Darklight (`Nyxorys`-aspected) | Lawful-biased gradient | Demonic Eve |
| Demonic Eve | Demonic | Darklight (`Nyxorys`-aspected) | Lawful-biased gradient | Demonic Adam |

Each is a **direct Creator-creation**. None of the four descend from any prior mortal; none of the four are elevations from any non-mortal source; none of the four are products of cross-lineage. They are made by the Creator, in pairs, in [Eden](../places/eden.md), and they are the only direct Creator-creations at the mortal tier.

## Mana-Flavor

Each First carries a **unique mana-flavor** as a direct Creator-creation. The four flavors are paired:

- **Humanic Adam-flavor** and **humanic Eve-flavor** are complementary aspects of light-affinity humanic mana.
- **Demonic Adam-flavor** and **demonic Eve-flavor** are complementary aspects of darklight-affinity demonic mana.

The exact contents of each flavor are open canon. What is locked: the four flavors are distinct from one another, distinct from `Solaryth` and `Nyxorys` directly, and serve as the cosmic anchor-points from which all mortal mana descends.

## Descent

Mortal lineages descend from the Firsts by **Creator-extension into the ordinary world**. The canonical mechanism is not "the Firsts left Eden and had children." The Firsts have not left Eden. The mechanism is that the Creator extended the lineage of each First into the mortal-world by direct creation of further mortals carrying the First's mana-flavor in dilute form. These further mortals are the ancestors of the mortal lineages mortal civilization knows.

This means:
- Every humanic mortal alive on Aeron carries dilute humanic-Adam-or-Eve mana-flavor.
- Every demonic mortal alive on Aeron carries dilute demonic-Adam-or-Eve mana-flavor.
- The cosmic origin of mortal life is in [Eden](../places/eden.md), traceable through generation-chains.

## Not Fallen

The Firsts have **not** fallen. There is no canonical fall-of-the-firsts doctrine in Aeron's mythopedia. The mortal lineages that descend from the Firsts did not descend by mortal sin or expulsion; they descend by Creator-extension. The Firsts remain blessed in [Eden](../places/eden.md), in eternal life, in unfallen state.

Mortal traditions sometimes import "fall" framings from outside-Aeron cosmologies; these are folk-confusion and not canon.

## Relations Among the Firsts

The Firsts coexist in [Eden](../places/eden.md). Their relations are canonically:

- **Within-pair (Adam-Eve):** the canonical pairing within each race; complementary, not antagonistic.
- **Across-race (humanic-demonic):** harmonious. The light/darklight affinity axis is not a moral split; humanic and demonic firsts are not in conflict in `Eden`.
- **With the Creator:** in direct relation. The Creator visits Eden.

The interior life of the Firsts — how they spend eternity, whether they have ongoing relation with their dilute descendants, whether they are aware of mortal events — is open canon.

## Cosmic Function

The Firsts function as:

1. **Direct Creator-creations at the mortal tier.** Their existence anchors the Creator's continuing presence at mortal level.
2. **Cosmic origin of mortal mana.** Every drop of mortal mana traces ancestry through them.
3. **The unfallen reference frame.** The Firsts are the mortal-tier example of beings who do not need redemption, do not require salvation-story arc, and do not function as victims of any cosmic catastrophe. They are simply alive, blessed, and undying.

## Hard Canon Locked by This Page

- The Firsts are exactly four: humanic Adam, humanic Eve, demonic Adam, demonic Eve.
- All four are direct Creator-creations.
- Each carries a unique mana-flavor.
- Mortal lineages descend by Creator-extension, not by Firsts leaving Eden.
- The Firsts have not fallen.
- The Firsts dwell in [Eden](../places/eden.md), unreachable to mortals.
- Humanic-demonic coexistence in Eden is canonical and harmonious.

## Canon Boundaries

- The proper primal-language headwords for each of the four Firsts are open canon. Naming may be set by future primal-language work.
- The visual rendering of the Firsts (appearance, dress, comportment) is open and varies by mortal tradition.
- The exact contents of each First's mana-flavor are open canon.
- Whether the Firsts have any ongoing intervention in mortal affairs (ancestral spirits called from Eden? prayers heard by Eden directly? sky-events traceable to Eden?) is open and largely closed off by the unreachability of Eden.
- Whether the Firsts have any role in the hero-elevation pathway into tier-4 godhood is open.
