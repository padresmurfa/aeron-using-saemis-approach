# Athravenel

- Rendered form: `Athravenel`
- Gloss: The Wild-Heart; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all woken-place and vegetal-sovereign kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Athravenel` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose body is land that woke up wrong. The progenitor of every haunted forest, malicious mountain, plant-titan, treant-class, and place-as-monster whose shape is geographical rather than animal.

## Scope

`Athravenel` carries:

- Cataclysmic woken-place sovereignty
- Vegetal and geographical-aspect cataclysm
- Progenitor-status for haunted forests, malicious mountains, plant-titans, treant-class, place-as-monster, woken-cave-system lineages
- Genius-loci-of-cataclysm: the spirit of a place raised to a level mortals can no longer ignore
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — distinctive for being read as a topography rather than as a creature

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Athravenel` is the beast that does not need to walk because it is already where you are. Its descended kin are places: the forest that has its own intent, the mountain whose route through it changes by the hour, the marsh whose boundary moves to keep travelers in. Forester-mages, geomancers, and wise-mortals who travel respect `Athravenel`'s line as one would respect any sovereign whose territory cannot be left.

## Boundaries

`Athravenel` must not be confused with [Drathalon](drathalon.md) (Colossus-Father), whose humanoid mass is mobile; `Athravenel`'s sovereignty is specifically place-bound. Walking-mountains belong to `Drathalon`; mountains-that-are-malicious-where-they-stand belong to `Athravenel`. The canonical test: does the body move on legs, or does the body move by extending its territory?

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Athravenel` belongs.
- [Drathalon](drathalon.md) (Colossus-Father) is the closest neighbor on the form-axis; the two are distinguished by mobility-mode.
- [Athral-](athral.md) family (existing structural principle root for layered structure) is etymologically related; `Athravenel` extends the family into living place.

## Note On Form

`Athravenel` blends the existing `Athr-` family (structural-architectonic, layered-form) with the new `-avenel` ending (woken-place, the territory-as-being). The word names a being whose existence is the rooting of structure in malice.
