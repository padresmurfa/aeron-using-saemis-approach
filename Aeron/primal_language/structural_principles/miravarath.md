# Miravarath

- Rendered form: `Miravarath`
- Gloss: The Burning South; the desert and volcanic continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Miravarath` names the southern continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the burning great-extent whose climate is dominated by sand, sun, and seismic volcanism, and whose mythic substrate is fire, hunger, and the burning-throne.

## Scope

`Miravarath` carries:
- Southern geographic position; hot climate; desert and volcanic biome
- Fire-and-thirst mortal civilization
- Hosting of [Vorthalen](vorthalen.md) (Hellspawn-Sire) and [Khoramor](khoramor.md) (Maw-Eternal)

## Planetary Use

`Miravarath` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning fire-magic, oath-against-flame, the conservation of water, the fortifying of caravans, and the demonologist mage-orders that contend with `Vorthalen`'s line at home territory. The [Tavrekal](tavrekal.md) moon (Ash) is canonically venerated here above other moons.

## Boundaries

`Miravarath` is **burning, not barren**. The continent is harsh but supports rich civilization; the Burning South has produced some of Aeron's deepest mortal philosophical traditions.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Miravarath` belongs.
- [Vorthalen](vorthalen.md) and [Khoramor](khoramor.md) dwell here.
- [Tavrekal](tavrekal.md) (Ash Moon) carries strong cultural resonance.

## Note On Form

`Miravarath` blends `Mira-` (transformation-pressure, confined-change) with `-varath` (burning-southern-pattern). The word names a great-extent under heat-pressure.
