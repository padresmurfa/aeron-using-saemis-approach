# Drovenrath

- Rendered form: `Drovenrath`
- Gloss: The Leviathan-Deep; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all abyssal and ocean-megafaunal kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Drovenrath` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) who dwells beneath the deepest reach of any sea. The progenitor of krakens, abyssal sea-monsters, deep-ones, ocean-megafauna, and every cataclysmic creature whose body is shaped by the pressure of leagues of water above it.

## Scope

`Drovenrath` carries:

- Cataclysmic abyssal might
- Progenitor-status for krakens, abyssal sea-monsters, deep-ones, ocean-megafauna, sea-serpents-of-the-deep
- Pressure-aspect: forms shaped by depths mortals cannot reach
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — most legible when seen reflected on still water

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Drovenrath` is the beast under the keel. Its dwelling is in an abyssal trench at one of the world's oceanic deeps; its breathing pulls the tides into shapes the moons alone cannot account for. Its descended kin populate the deep oceans: the kraken that climbs reefs at midnight; the deep-one cult-spawning offshore of forgotten coasts; the leviathan-class beasts whose bodies drift through the sunless layers and whose corpses, when they wash ashore once a generation, kingdoms make legend of.

## Boundaries

`Drovenrath` must not be confused with [Velkoraen](velkoraen.md) (Wyrm-Sire) — Velkoraen's serpentine kin are coiled-elemental, often hoarding, often air-aspected; `Drovenrath`'s deep-kin are pressure-shaped, abyssal, and aware of the dark in ways that surface-creatures are not. Sea-serpents may belong to either lineage, but the canonical distinction is: a sea-serpent that hoards is `Velkoraen`'s; a sea-serpent that lurks is `Drovenrath`'s.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Drovenrath` belongs.
- [Velkoraen](velkoraen.md) (Wyrm-Sire) shares serpentine surface but is distinct in ontology.

## Note On Form

`Drovenrath` blends the new `Drov-` family (abyssal-deep, the dark-pressure-below) with the `-enrath` ending (oceanic-pattern, leagued-depth). The word's heaviness is intentional; the name itself sinks.
