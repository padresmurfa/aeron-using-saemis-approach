# Krevoraen

- Rendered form: `Krevoraen`
- Gloss: The Bloodborn-Sire; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all cursed-humanoid and blood-cursed kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Krevoraen` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose curse takes humanoid form and turns it predator. The progenitor of every vampire, werewolf, ghoul, wendigo, curse-bound humanoid champion, and blood-cursed lineage that retains a humanoid silhouette while losing the rest of mortal kinship.

## Scope

`Krevoraen` carries:

- Cataclysmic blood-curse; the body remains man-shaped, the rest does not
- Progenitor-status for vampires, werewolves, ghouls, wendigos, curse-bound humanoid champions, blood-cursed lineages, predator-mortals-who-are-no-longer-mortal
- Mirror-aspect: the descended kin look like a mortal at first glance, and that resemblance is the danger
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — folk-memory describes it as the silhouette of a person reshaped

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Krevoraen` is the beast whose kin pass for mortals until they cannot. Its descended lineages populate every continent; `Krevoraen`-class predators learn to be invisible because their hunting-ground is mortal civilization, and visibility is fatal. Hunter-orders, blood-mage academies, and wise-elder traditions in every culture record encounters with `Krevoraen`'s line; the cataclysm is rarely city-shaking but is constant and personal.

## Boundaries

`Krevoraen` is **categorically distinct from the humanic mortal race** at tier 6. The humanic mortals descend from a Creator-created Adam/Eve at the unreachable Eden and are a light-affinity humanoid race. `Krevoraen`'s kin are tier-5 cataclysmic beasts: cursed-humanoid predators with no necessary connection to humanic mortals or to [Solaryth](solaryth.md). The shared "humanoid" body-shape in mortal speech is a folk-conflation; the canonical lines are wholly separate. A humanic mortal is not a child of `Krevoraen`.

`Krevoraen` must also not be confused with [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign), whose deathless rulers wear the trappings of past mortal kingship in deathless form. `Krevoraen`'s kin are alive — undead-alive in many cases, but breathing in mortal patterns and descending by mortal generations. Vampires of `Krevoraen`'s line live, drink, breed; liches of `Skaelmorath`'s line do not.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Krevoraen` belongs.
- [Solaryth](solaryth.md) is the light-luminary that gives the humanic mortal race its affinity; `Krevoraen` is unrelated to this lineage despite the shared humanoid body-shape.
- [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign) and [Vorthalen](vorthalen.md) (Hellspawn-Sire) are the canonical adjacent `Vorothar`; together with `Krevoraen` they form the "shadow trio" of cataclysmic beasts often confused by untrained mortals but ontologically distinct.

## Note On Form

`Krevoraen` blends the new `Krev-` family (blood-curse, the rite that crosses mortal blood-line) with the `-oraen` augmentative (ancient-bearer, deep-of-time, paralleling the same suffix in [Velkoraen](velkoraen.md)). The shared `-oraen` ending with `Velkoraen` is intentional: both are progenitors whose lineages predate any single mortal generation and whose descended kin number in the multitudes.
