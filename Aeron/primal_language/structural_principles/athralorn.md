# Athralorn

- Rendered form: `Athralorn`
- Gloss: The Heartland; the central, stable, multi-kingdom continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Athralorn` names the central continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the great structural mainland whose stable geography, mild climate, and multiple cohabiting mortal kingdoms make it the canonical "main continent" for mortal-civic narrative.

## Scope

`Athralorn` carries:
- Central geographic position
- Stable, varied, multi-biome landscape
- Multi-kingdom mortal civilization
- Hosting of [Drathalon](drathalon.md) (Colossus-Father) and [Aelvorath](aelvorath.md) (Stormwing)

## Planetary Use

`Athralorn` is the canonical setting for most cross-cultural mortal-civic narrative on Aeron. It is large, varied, and contains the broadest population spectrum across kingdoms and races.

## Boundaries

`Athralorn` is **central in geography, not in cosmic hierarchy**. It is not "the canonical real continent" with the others as periphery; the [seven continents](../../mythopedia/places/continents.md) are co-equal in cosmic standing.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Athralorn` belongs.
- [Drathalon](drathalon.md) and [Aelvorath](aelvorath.md) dwell here.

## Note On Form

`Athralorn` blends `Athr-` (structural, layered) with `-alorn` (heartland-form). The word names a stable structured great-extent at the heart of the world.
