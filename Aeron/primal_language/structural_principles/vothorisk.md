# Vothorisk

- Rendered form: `Vothorisk`
- Gloss: The Eldritch-Eye; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all alien-geometry and cosmic-horror kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Vothorisk` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose form does not fit the world. Whose body is not a body; whose direction is not a direction; whose gaze is the wrong way around. The progenitor of every shoggoth, mind-eater, beholder-class, and thing-that-should-not-be in Aeron's bestiary.

## Scope

`Vothorisk` carries:

- Cataclysmic unfittedness; the geometry of cosmic dread
- Power that disorients rather than overpowers
- Progenitor-status for shoggoths, mind-eaters, beholder-class, alien-form beasts, things-that-should-not-be
- The dread-aspect: gazing on `Vothorisk`'s kin is itself injurious to mortal mind unless mana and attunement are sufficient
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — paradoxically, the constellation whose shape no astrologer agrees on

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Vothorisk` is the beast of cosmic horror. It is dormant in a way the other `Vorothar` are not — not asleep, not absent, but **not aligned with the world's directions**. Its descended kin appear in places where the world's lawful geometry has thinned: deep ocean trenches, mind-mage academies that asked the wrong questions, lost cities whose foundations were laid wrong.

## Boundaries

`Vothorisk` must not be confused with the [Vatharen](vatharen.md) (tier-3 forces are felt as substance, not as cosmic dread; a `Vatharen` does not break perception), nor with personal demonic gods at tier 4 (those are personalities; `Vothorisk` is barely a personality at all). `Vothorisk` is also not the cause of mortal madness in general; only its kin in close presence carry the dread-aspect, and only at sufficient proximity.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Vothorisk` belongs.
- [Khaethorom](khaethorom.md) (Form-Thief) shares some surface adjacency but is concerned with deception of form, not break of geometry.

## Note On Form

`Vothorisk` blends the new `Voth-` family (the unknowable; the unfit; what does not align) with the new `-orisk` ending (alien-presence, cosmic-foreign). The word is itself slightly unsettling to native primal-language speakers, who note its phonemes do not braid as cleanly as most beast-headwords. This is held to be intentional: the name names what does not fit by not quite fitting.
