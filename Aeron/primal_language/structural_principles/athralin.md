# Athralin

- Rendered form: `Athralin`
- Gloss: continent; the great structural extents of [Aeron](aeron.md)
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Athralin` names the great structural extents of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the seven canonical landmasses upon which mortal civilization, [Vorothar](vorothar.md) dwellings, and pantheon-worship traditions are arrayed. It is the principle by which a swath of Aeron's surface coheres as a distinct land with continuity of geography, climate, and mythic resonance.

## Scope

`Athralin` carries:

- Continent-scale extent
- Geographical, climatic, and biotic continuity
- A bounded surface for hosted myth (Vorothar dwellings, pantheon-worship traditions, mortal civilization)
- Universal cosmic existence with continent-local content

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier framework, `Athralin` are the seven canonical continents: [Athralorn](athralorn.md) (Heartland), [Kethrarvel](kethrarvel.md) (Frostbound North), [Miravarath](miravarath.md) (Burning South), [Phaeravar](phaeravar.md) (Eastern Ancient), [Seralinor](seralinor.md) (Sea-Threading), [Tavorath](tavorath.md) (High Crown), and [Oraevenel](oraevenel.md) (Lost Verge). Each is a distinct land with its own character, its own hosted [Vorothar](vorothar.md), and (where mortal civilization has developed) its own [pantheon-worship tradition](../../mythopedia/cosmology/pantheons.md).

## Boundaries

`Athralin` must not be confused with [Aeron](aeron.md) itself (Aeron is the world; `Athralin` are its continents) or with Aeron's tectonic-plate inventory (a planetary-physics concern, separate from the mythopedia canon of named continents). A continent in mythopedia terms is the named, mythic landform; a tectonic plate is a geological structure.

## Relations

- [Aeron](aeron.md) is the world that contains the `Athralin`.
- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) dwell on `Athralin`; each continent hosts two Vorothar.
- [Tavirath](tavirath.md) constellations are positioned over Aeron's geocentric-round sky and are read brightest from each `Athralin` according to which Vorothar dwell there.

## Note On Form

`Athralin` blends the existing `Athr-` family (structural-architectonic, layered-form) with the new `-alin` ending (great-extent, continent-class). The word names a bounded structural extent of the world.
