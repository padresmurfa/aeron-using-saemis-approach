# Phaelvenor

- Rendered form: `Phaelvenor`
- Gloss: The Swarm-Voice; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all many-as-one collective kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Phaelvenor` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose body is many bodies and whose voice is many voices. The progenitor of every locust-god, gestalt-collective, devouring-swarm, and many-mouthed cataclysm whose answer to a single question is delivered in a thousand simultaneous mouths.

## Scope

`Phaelvenor` carries:

- Cataclysmic collectivity; many-as-one as cosmic principle
- Progenitor-status for locust-gods, devil-swarms, gestalt-collectives, many-mouthed beasts, hive-cataclysms, plague-of-many lineages
- Aggregate-aspect: no single body of `Phaelvenor`'s kin is large; the cataclysm is in the count
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — itself rendered as a galaxy-like cluster of small lights, easily mistaken for a distant pantheon

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Phaelvenor` is the beast you cannot fight by killing one. Its descended kin populate cataclysmic event-zones where the count itself is the threat: the locust-cloud that darkens the sun for a season, the devouring-swarm that consumes a forest in a single night, the hive-mind that speaks with a single voice through the mouths of its many.

## Boundaries

`Phaelvenor` must not be confused with the [Phaedren](phaedren.md), the existing harmonic intelligence of phase-correction; their roots share the `Phae-` family but their tiers and ontologies are wholly distinct. `Phaedren` is a corrective intelligence in service of cosmic harmony; `Phaelvenor` is a cataclysmic beast.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Phaelvenor` belongs.
- [Phaedren](phaedren.md), [Phaemoral](phaemoral.md), [Phaemorath](phaemorath.md) share the `Phae-` family but are not beasts.

## Note On Form

`Phaelvenor` blends the existing `Phae-` family (many-as-one, harmonic-aggregate) with the new `-venor` ending (many-bearer, host-agent). The word names the principle of swarm-as-being: the beast whose unity is its multiplicity.
