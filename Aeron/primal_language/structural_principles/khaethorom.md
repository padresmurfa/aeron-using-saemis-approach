# Khaethorom

- Rendered form: `Khaethorom`
- Gloss: The Form-Thief; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all shapeshifter and mimic kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Khaethorom` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose own body has no settled shape. The progenitor of every doppelganger, mimic, changeling, skinwalker, and formless-thing whose nature is to wear a body it did not make.

## Scope

`Khaethorom` carries:

- Cataclysmic formlessness; identity-as-deception
- Progenitor-status for doppelgangers, mimics, changelings, skinwalkers, formless-things
- Theft-aspect: the kin do not merely change form; they take form from another
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — its outline never-quite-the-same on any two nights, leading some astrologers to mistake it for a fated-being star in flux

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Khaethorom` is the beast that may already be present. Its descended kin populate the spaces of mortal trust most easily betrayed: the village newcomer, the diplomat at court, the spouse who returned from war slightly different. Mage-orders dedicated to identification and authentication trace their founding to early human contact with `Khaethorom`'s line.

## Boundaries

`Khaethorom` must not be confused with [Mirathven](mirathven.md) (Chimera-Mother), whose offspring have stable hybrid bodies. `Khaethorom`'s offspring have no body that holds for long; the shape they wear is borrowed, not braided.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Khaethorom` belongs.
- [Mirathven](mirathven.md) (Chimera-Mother) is the canonical contrast: stable hybrid form vs unstable borrowed form.

## Note On Form

`Khaethorom` blends the new `Khaeth-` family (unfixed-form; the body that does not hold) with the new `-orom` ending (many-bearer, the wearer-of-faces). The word names a being whose primal-language headword is itself slightly slippery — speakers report it sometimes feels like a different word in their mouth than in their ear.
