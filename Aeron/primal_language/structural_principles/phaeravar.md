# Phaeravar

- Rendered form: `Phaeravar`
- Gloss: The Eastern Ancient; the deeply-historied, mythic continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Phaeravar` names the eastern continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the most deeply-historied of the seven great-extents, whose civilization is older than any other continent's living mortal record, and whose mythic substrate is memory, witness, and the unfinished cosmic geometry that does not always agree with the rest of the world.

## Scope

`Phaeravar` carries:
- Eastern geographic position; varied climate; long-history landscape
- Ancient mortal civilization with continuous record across many ages
- Hosting of [Vothorisk](vothorisk.md) (Eldritch-Eye) and [Phaelvenor](phaelvenor.md) (Swarm-Voice)

## Planetary Use

`Phaeravar` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning ancient lineage, archived memory, and the dread-traditions that arise from prolonged contact with `Vothorisk`'s line. The continent's long mage-academies trace founding to encounters with cosmic geometry that resists ordinary mortal cartography.

## Boundaries

`Phaeravar` is **ancient, not stagnant**. The Eastern Ancient continues to produce new mortal civilization, art, and mage-craft; its weight is the weight of accumulated centuries, not torpor.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Phaeravar` belongs.
- [Vothorisk](vothorisk.md) and [Phaelvenor](phaelvenor.md) dwell here.
- The [Phae-](phaedren.md) family is etymologically resonant; `Phaeravar` and the memory-witnessing intelligences share a phonemic-family root.

## Note On Form

`Phaeravar` blends `Phae-` (memory, witnessing intelligence) with `-avar` (ancient-eastern). The word names a great-extent of accumulated time.
