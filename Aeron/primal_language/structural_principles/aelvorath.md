# Aelvorath

- Rendered form: `Aelvorath`
- Gloss: The Stormwing; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all sky-predator and tempest-bearing kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Aelvorath` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose wing carries the storm. The progenitor of every roc, thunderbird, garuda-class, and tempest-bringer whose passage is mistaken for weather and whose weather is mistaken for prophecy.

## Scope

`Aelvorath` carries:

- Cataclysmic aerial predation
- Tempest-bearing might; the storm is part of the body
- Progenitor-status for rocs, thunderbirds, garuda-class, tempest-bringers, sky-cataclysm-of-feather lineages
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky, often misread by mortals as a weather-pattern about to arrive

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Aelvorath` flies seasons. Its dwelling is in the upper-atmosphere thermals over a single continent, but its presence is felt across hemispheres because the storms it sheds travel ahead of it and behind it. Mortal storm-cults venerate `Aelvorath` as the maker of certain great storm-eras, and pilots and sky-mages read the angle of its constellation as a weather-omen at multi-decade scale.

## Boundaries

`Aelvorath` must not be confused with the [Vatharen](vatharen.md) `Taelmoth` (Strife), whose friction is felt as substance and is not a flying body. Nor with the seventh moon `Taviril` (Blue Moon), whose air-aspect is distance-made-intimate, not predation-by-storm. `Aelvorath` is a beast: a predator that flies and whose flight makes weather.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Aelvorath` belongs.
- [Taviril](taviril.md) (Blue Moon) is air-as-distance; `Aelvorath` is air-as-predator. Distinct.

## Note On Form

`Aelvorath` blends the new `Ael-` family (sky-storm, the air that carries weight) with the `-vorath` ending (forward-pattern, the borne-onward shape). The word names a being whose flight is itself the omen of its arrival.
