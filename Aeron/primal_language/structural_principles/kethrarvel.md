# Kethrarvel

- Rendered form: `Kethrarvel`
- Gloss: The Frostbound North; the cold, fortified, oath-and-iron continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Kethrarvel` names the northern continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the frostbound great-extent whose climate is shaped by long winter, whose civilization is fortified against the cold and against what dwells beneath the ice, and whose mythic substrate is iron, oath, and the wraith-throne of the deep frost.

## Scope

`Kethrarvel` carries:
- Northern geographic position; cold climate; frost-aspected biome
- Fortified, oath-bound mortal civilization
- Hosting of [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign) and [Velkoraen](velkoraen.md) (Wyrm-Sire)

## Planetary Use

`Kethrarvel` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning fortification, lasting oath, the keeping of long-cold things, and the dynastic survival of kingdoms across hard winters. The local mortal traditions are heavily oath-and-iron influenced; the [Kethorel](kethorel.md) moon is canonically venerated here above other moons.

## Boundaries

`Kethrarvel` is **frostbound, not lifeless**. The continent is harsh but inhabited; mortal civilization here is canonically resilient rather than sparse.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Kethrarvel` belongs.
- [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) and [Velkoraen](velkoraen.md) dwell here.
- [Kethorel](kethorel.md) (Iron Moon) carries strong cultural resonance with `Kethrarvel`'s mortal traditions.

## Note On Form

`Kethrarvel` blends `Keth-` (enclosure, oath, iron) with `-arvel` (frost-northern). The word names a great-extent under cold rule.
