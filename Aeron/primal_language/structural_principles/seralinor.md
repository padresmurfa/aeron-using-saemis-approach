# Seralinor

- Rendered form: `Seralinor`
- Gloss: The Sea-Threading; the archipelago/ocean-spanning maritime continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Seralinor` names the archipelago-continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — a great-extent composed of many islands threaded by sea-lanes rather than a single landmass, and whose mythic substrate is water-cycle, blood-line continuity, and the deep cosmic patience of those who live where land is the exception.

## Scope

`Seralinor` carries:
- Archipelago geography; many islands, many sea-lanes
- Maritime mortal civilization
- Hosting of [Drovenrath](drovenrath.md) (Leviathan-Deep) and [Krevoraen](krevoraen.md) (Bloodborn-Sire)

## Planetary Use

`Seralinor` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning sea-trade, navigation, blood-cursed island-dynasties, deep-water mage-orders, and the long cultural memory of port-cities. The [Serenel](serenel.md)-[Seramor](seramor.md) water axis is canonically central to mortal life here.

## Boundaries

`Seralinor` is a **continent in the canonical sense** despite being archipelago-form. The cosmic-cohesion of the islands is a single `Athralin`; mortals who live across the islands are one continental civilization, not many.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Seralinor` belongs.
- [Drovenrath](drovenrath.md) and [Krevoraen](krevoraen.md) dwell here.
- [Serenel](serenel.md) and [Seramor](seramor.md) carry deep cultural resonance.

## Note On Form

`Seralinor` blends `Sera-` (water, cycle) with `-linor` (linked-isle). The word names a great-extent threaded by sea.
