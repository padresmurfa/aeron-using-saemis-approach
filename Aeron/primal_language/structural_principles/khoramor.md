# Khoramor

- Rendered form: `Khoramor`
- Gloss: The Maw-Eternal; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all hunger-incarnate kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Khoramor` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose form is appetite given body. The progenitor of every world-eater, tarrasque-class glutton, and hunger-spirit too immense to be a hunger of any particular thing. `Khoramor` does not eat to live; `Khoramor` is the eating that the world endures.

## Scope

`Khoramor` carries:

- Cataclysmic appetite; hunger that does not stop
- Progenitor-status for tarrasques, world-eaters, gluttons-of-cosmic-scale, behir-class, devouring beasts whose only end is consumption
- Maw-aspect: the central feature is the mouth, the throat, the swallowing
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — visible most clearly during long famines

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Khoramor` is the beast that has eaten cities, swallowed armies, drained inland seas, and then slept satisfied for an age before stirring again. Its descended kin are smaller hungers: tarrasques that ravage continents, gluttons that empty granaries, predators that cannot be sated. Mortals fear `Khoramor`'s line because its hunger does not pause for negotiation.

## Boundaries

`Khoramor` must not be confused with the [Vatharen](vatharen.md) `Yirmor` (Decay) — `Yirmor` is slow unmaking as cosmic substance; `Khoramor` is appetite as a body that walks. Nor with `Velkoraen`'s wyrm-kin, who often hoard rather than consume. Nor with the existential hunger sometimes named at tier 1 in pre-creation lore; `Khoramor` is post-creation hunger, a beast and not a metaphysics.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Khoramor` belongs.
- [Yirmor](yirmor.md) (Decay) is the tier-3 force that runs adjacent; `Khoramor` is its embodied beast-counterpart at tier 5, distinct in ontology.

## Note On Form

`Khoramor` blends the new `Khor-` family (hunger-deep, appetite-without-end) with the `-amor` ending (depth, the not-having that draws). The word names a being whose name itself sounds like a swallow — the syllables draw downward.
