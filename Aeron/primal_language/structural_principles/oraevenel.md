# Oraevenel

- Rendered form: `Oraevenel`
- Gloss: The Lost Verge; the small, liminal, half-mapped, dangerous continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Oraevenel` names the verge-continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — the smallest of the seven `Athralin`, whose geography does not stably agree with mortal cartography, and which is **the dormant body of [Athravenel](athravenel.md) (Wild-Heart)**. The continent and the Vorothar are the same being viewed from two angles: `Oraevenel` is the territory; `Athravenel` is the territory's awareness.

## Scope

`Oraevenel` carries:
- Verge geography; small, half-mapped, liminal
- Cartography that does not stably agree across visits
- The continent IS Athravenel's body (canonical singular case)
- Hosting of [Khaethorom](khaethorom.md) (Form-Thief, at the unmappable edge) within Athravenel's larger territory

## Planetary Use

`Oraevenel` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning genius-loci tradition, druidic mage-orders, the authentication of identity at the edge of the world, and the hunting of `Khaethorom`'s line in territory whose geography itself participates in the deception. Mortal civilization here is sparse, ancient, and canonically careful.

## Boundaries

`Oraevenel` is **liminal, not non-existent**. The continent is real and mortal-inhabited at small scale; its unmappability is structural to its nature, not an erasure.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Oraevenel` belongs.
- [Athravenel](athravenel.md) (Wild-Heart) **is** this continent.
- [Khaethorom](khaethorom.md) (Form-Thief) dwells at the unmappable edges.
- [Oraemor](oraemor.md) (Pale Moon, threshold) carries deep cultural resonance — the moon and the continent share a thematic root family.

## Note On Form

`Oraevenel` blends `Orae-` (threshold, passage) with `-venel` (verge-bearer). The word names a great-extent that is itself a threshold.
