# Tavorath

- Rendered form: `Tavorath`
- Gloss: The High Crown; the high-altitude, mountainous, sky-temple continent of Aeron
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Tavorath` names the high-altitude continent of [Aeron](aeron.md) — a great-extent whose elevation is dominated by alpine ranges and high plateaus, whose air is canonically thin and venerable, and whose mythic substrate is sky-attention, hybrid-form (the labyrinth-territory of [Mirathven](mirathven.md)), and the cloud-marsh sovereignty of cosmic plague.

## Scope

`Tavorath` carries:
- High-altitude geography; alpine and plateau biomes
- Sky-temple culture; mortal civilization oriented toward the heavens
- Hosting of [Mirathven](mirathven.md) (Chimera-Mother) and [Vesselrath](vesselrath.md) (Plague-Mother in the cloud-marsh)

## Planetary Use

`Tavorath` is the canonical setting for mortal narrative concerning sky-temple religion, alpine pilgrimage, paradox-keeper traditions (the chimera-cult resonance of `Mirathven`), and cloud-marsh sorrow-cults. Mortal mage-orders here often work primarily with [Taviril](taviril.md) (Blue Moon, distance) mana.

## Boundaries

`Tavorath` is **high, not unreachable**. Mortal civilization lives here; the continent is venerable but inhabited.

## Relations

- [Athralin](athralin.md) is the class to which `Tavorath` belongs.
- [Mirathven](mirathven.md) and [Vesselrath](vesselrath.md) dwell here.
- [Taviril](taviril.md) (Blue Moon) carries strong cultural resonance.

## Note On Form

`Tavorath` blends `Tav-` (sky, radiance) with `-orath` (great-pattern, paralleling `Tavirath`). The word names a great-extent that draws toward sky.
