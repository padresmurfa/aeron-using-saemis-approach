# Velkoraen

- Rendered form: `Velkoraen`
- Gloss: The Wyrm-Sire; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all serpentine and dragon kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Velkoraen` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) who is the coiled ancient at the head of every serpent-class and dragon-class lineage in Aeron. The being whose constellation rings the sky in slow scales, whose dwelling continent is marked by the great hoard-cradle, and whose dreams are the dragons.

## Scope

`Velkoraen` carries:

- Coiled cataclysmic might
- Elemental and ancestral serpentine power
- Progenitor-status for dragons, sea-serpents, hydras, lung, tatzelwurm, and the jormungandr-class world-coilers
- Hoard-mind: the keeping of long-thought treasure as cosmic principle
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Velkoraen` is the first-named `Vorothar` and the most folk-recurrent of all cataclysmic beasts. Children of `Velkoraen` populate the world: every dragon, every wyrm, every serpentine cataclysm bears `Velkoraen`'s dilute mana-flavor.

## Boundaries

`Velkoraen` must not be confused with [Drovenrath](drovenrath.md) (Leviathan-Deep, the abyssal-oceanic `Vorothar`, whose serpentine kin are sea-of-the-deep rather than coiled-elemental), or with mortal serpent-cults that worship lesser dragons. `Velkoraen` is not a god; it does not belong to any galaxy. It is a beast: meetable, fightable in extremis, gazable, and aware.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Velkoraen` belongs.
- [Drovenrath](drovenrath.md) is the abyssal `Vorothar`; their lineages cross only in rare hybrid kin.
- [Tavirath](tavirath.md) is the constellation-class within which `Velkoraen`'s sky-pattern resides.

## Note On Form

`Velkoraen` blends the new `Vel-` family (coiled-power, slow-strength, ringed enclosure) with the `-koraen` augmentative (ancient-bearer, deep-of-time). The word names a being whose great age is part of its cataclysmic weight; the wyrm is not just coiled but old past mortal reckoning.
