# Skaelmorath

- Rendered form: `Skaelmorath`
- Gloss: The Wraith-Sovereign; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all death-aspected sovereign kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Skaelmorath` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) who rules from the other side of dying. Crowned in pale fire and grave-dust; the progenitor of every lich, dread-knight, banshee-queen, dread-spectre, and nazgul-class deathless ruler.

## Scope

`Skaelmorath` carries:

- Cataclysmic sovereignty in deathless form
- Progenitor-status for liches, dread-knights, banshees, dread-spectres, nazgul-class, all undead-aspect ruler lineages
- The cold-throne: rule from the place where breath is not required
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — visible chiefly in the deep hours of night

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Skaelmorath` is the beast that rules. The body is unbreathing, the face is veiled, the hand is still ringed with the metal of an ancient kingship. Its descended kin take that pattern in many forms: the lich who outlives every dynasty he served, the dread-knight whose vows survived his flesh, the banshee-queen whose grief is itself a sovereign command. None of them are `Yirmor` or `Yirven` — none are decay or growth as substance. They are beings that have crossed the breath-edge and come back wearing crowns.

## Boundaries

`Skaelmorath` must not be confused with the [Vatharen](vatharen.md) `Yirmor` (Decay), nor with the personal gods of any pantheon (whose deathless office is granted by Creator-creation or by hero-elevation, not by passing through the death-edge). Nor with mere ghosts, who are tier-6 spirits and lack sovereignty. Nor with `Vesselrath` (Plague-Mother), whose kin produce the deaths but do not rule from beyond them.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Skaelmorath` belongs.
- [Yirmor](yirmor.md) (Decay) is the tier-3 force; `Skaelmorath` is the embodied beast. Their domains touch but are tier-distinct.
- [Vesselrath](vesselrath.md) (Plague-Mother) parents disease-bearing kin, distinct from `Skaelmorath`'s deathless rulers.

## Note On Form

`Skaelmorath` blends the new `Skael-` family (death-pale, the breath-after-breath) with the `-morath` ending (depth-pattern, the layered-sovereignty). The word names a being whose name itself feels cold to the speaker.
