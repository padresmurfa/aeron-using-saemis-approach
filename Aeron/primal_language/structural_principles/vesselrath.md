# Vesselrath

- Rendered form: `Vesselrath`
- Gloss: The Plague-Mother; Cataclysmic Beast progenitor of all pestilence and contagion kin
- Tier: structural principle

## Meaning

`Vesselrath` names the [Vorothar](vorothar.md) whose body carries the rot the world gives back. The progenitor of every pest-beast, contagion-spirit, rot-bearer, and plague-walker whose passage is followed by sickness and whose body is itself a vessel of sickness made meat.

## Scope

`Vesselrath` carries:

- Cataclysmic pestilence
- Vessel-aspect: the body is the bearer; the contagion is offered, not flung
- Progenitor-status for pest-beasts, contagion-spirits, rot-bearers, plague-walkers, beast-of-the-sores lineages
- One of the fourteen most prominent constellations in the sky — dim under most conditions; brightening visibly during great epidemics

## Planetary Use

In Aeron's tier-5 canon, `Vesselrath` is the beast that does not need to attack. Mortal contact with its descended kin is sufficient: the sickness propagates of itself. Plague-mage cults learn to read `Vesselrath`'s constellation for the seasons of greatest danger; physicians and rot-priests call upon its name in oaths of confinement.

## Boundaries

`Vesselrath` must not be confused with [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign), whose deathless rulers cause death-by-rule, not death-by-rot. Nor with `Yirmor` (the [Vatharen](vatharen.md) of Decay), whose unmaking is felt as cosmic substance and is not a beast at all. `Vesselrath`'s kin are alive, breathing, and contagious; they suffer the rot they bear.

## Relations

- [Vorothar](vorothar.md) is the class to which `Vesselrath` belongs.
- [Skaelmorath](skaelmorath.md) (Wraith-Sovereign) parents deathless rulers; `Vesselrath` parents living plague-bearers. Distinct.
- [Yirmor](yirmor.md) (Decay) is the tier-3 force; `Vesselrath` is the embodied beast.

## Note On Form

`Vesselrath` blends the new `Vess-` family (vessel-of-rot; the body-as-bearer) with the `-elrath` ending (mother-pattern, the giving-forth). The word names a being whose mothering is exactly what mortals fear about her.
