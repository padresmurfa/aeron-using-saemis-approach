## Findings

### Solaryth (luminary)

**Status:** Clean. Aligned with new spine doctrine — `Solaryth` is named as light-affinity luminary, properly distinguished from the Seravel by use of `Taviran`, and depicted in non-moral terms (exacting rather than benevolent). No contradictions.

### Nyxorys (luminary)

**Status:** Clean. Saga is structurally an exemplar of the new doctrine. Phrase "the timid call it cursed, but the wise do not" anticipates the spine canon that the light/darklight axis is non-moral. Fully compatible with the new [`light_darklight_affinity.md`](../../Aeron/mythopedia/cosmology/light_darklight_affinity.md).

### Seven moon sagas (Serenel, Seramor, Tavrekal, Taviril, Kethorel, Oraemor, Serathil)

**Status:** Clean (after one cleanup). Each saga reads as an evocation of the moon's character that distills cleanly into the mana-flavor articulations added at Step 3 (`moons_of_aeron.md`). The narrative team's voice in the sagas is more evocative than the canon page's flavor articulation; the two are complementary rather than conflicting.

**One cleanup required and applied:** `oraemor.md` opened with "no longer counted among the blunt elemental powers." This was a transitional artifact from a prior canon revision (the original elemental-matrix framing). Step 3 fully retired the elemental-matrix framing in `moons_of_aeron.md`. The transitional clause is now removed; the rest of the saga is unchanged and reads clean.

### Phaemorath (Memory Constellations class)

**Status:** Clean. Saga frames `Phaemorath` as the constellation-class for "remembrance learned to endure as arrangement," fully compatible with both:
- The new spine's tier-5 doctrine (constellations are real arrangements with cosmic significance).
- The new R2 stars doctrine (Class 1 lexical-witness stars provide the substrate against which Class 2 fated-being stars are read; Phaemorath specifically extends memory-witnessing to constellary scale).

No contradictions. The saga's metaphor "history, once borne high enough, becomes mercy for those who arrive too late to witness the first beginning" is canonically beautiful and does not require revision.

### Taviron (Entangled Constellations class)

**Status:** Clean. Saga's framing of `Taviron` as "the old answerability of the heavens made visible in pattern" is structurally aligned with the [Star-Fate Doctrine R2 + positional-weighting](../../Aeron/mythopedia/astrology/star_fate.md), particularly the line "lights that ought to have become strangers still answer one another." This anticipates the positional-weighting mechanic of Class 2 stars clustering near their fate-words.

The saga remains framework-clean. It can be read with the new doctrine and gain richness rather than contradiction.

The fourteen Vorothar are a new tier-5 cohort introduced in Step 6a/6b and currently have only mythopedia canon entries, not saga prose. **Saga prose for the fourteen Vorothar is downstream narrative work** and is not in this audit's scope.
- Pantheon sagas, hero-tier sagas, mortal-tier sagas, and Vatharen sagas similarly do not yet exist; these are downstream narrative work.
